# Epi Eightfold Experiential Vision

## 0. Why this distinction now exists

The M′ corpus contains two different forms of depth that had been allowed to overlap in product language:

1. the **3:3 composition** in which M1/M2/M3 form the Cosmic face and M4/M5/M0 form the Personal face; and
2. the **recursive 4+2 grammar** inside every individual M′ domain, where the inner four dynamics are held by a 0/5 framing pair and can open into a complete instrument of their own.

These are not two descriptions of the same UI hierarchy.

The 3:3 relation answers: **what whole Epi world is the person inhabiting now?**

The 4+2 relation answers: **what does this one M′ domain become when entered at its own native depth?**

The application therefore has an **eightfold product field**:

```text
COMPOSED PRODUCTS

  [A] Cosmic 1/2/3
      one situated cosmic instrument / world

  [B] Personal 4/5/0
      one situated lived / reflective / dialogical world

DEEP PRODUCTS

  [0] M0′ Anuttara deep instrument
  [1] M1′ Paramaśiva deep instrument
  [2] M2′ Paraśakti deep instrument
  [3] M3′ Mahāmāyā deep instrument
  [4] M4′ Nara deep instrument
  [5] M5′ Epii deep instrument
```

The word *eightfold* is product language, not a new mathematical coordinate system. It does not add M6 or M7. It names **two compositions plus six native depths**.

---

## 1. The two composed products are whole experiences

### 1.1 Cosmic 1/2/3

The Cosmic product is the everyday/current expression of the relation among:

- **M1 Paramaśiva** — current act, pulse, QL movement and topology;
- **M2 Paraśakti** — current world-condition, harmonic/correspondential field and live solar relation;
- **M3 Mahāmāyā** — current inscription, clock, symbolic matter and state-signature.

It is not a dashboard containing three subsystem widgets. It is one situated event seen cosmically.

The recovered Cycle-3 sources already require composition over juxtaposition, one shared tick/profile, a solar-system spatial anchor, Earth as observer-centre, a played K²/QL topology, and a 360°/720° Mahāmāyā clock field. The present vision develops those commitments into one world rather than choosing one old renderer sketch as the whole product.

The exact parent-scale visual language may continue to evolve, but the law is fixed: **the Cosmic product must be experientially complete without becoming the full deep M1′, M2′ or M3′ instrument.**

### 1.2 Personal 4/5/0

The Personal product is the everyday lived Epi environment. Its primary activities are deliberately ordinary and useful:

- journalling and daily writing;
- a DAY/NOW dashboard or canvas;
- kanban / activity / flow arrangements where they serve the day;
- note, episode and document work;
- dialogical engagement with Epii through the real Agent/AgentSession relation;
- oracle services and symbolic readings;
- review, provenance, explanation and return;
- navigation from lived concern into Bimba/source/canon when useful.

This is **not** the same thing as the deep M4′ psychoid instrument.

The Nara source corpus contains a rich protected psychoid body: quaternionic composition, sushumna/chakra assemblage, elemental field, Hopf-linked tori, cymatic perturbation, identity evidence, trajectory, somatic/oracular/lived integration. That material now has a proper home: **the deep M4′ product**.

The Personal 4/5/0 product can use any of those capabilities when they help the current activity, but it does not need to render the complete deep organism continuously. A journal can remain a journal. A day canvas can remain a day canvas. An oracle can open its own native reading UI. A dialogue can remain a canonical Agent encounter. Their unity comes from the same governed Personal subject and current event, not from forcing every capability into one permanent visual scene.

This distinction allows Nara to become both simpler **and** deeper:

```text
Personal 4/5/0
    practical lived surface
    writing / day / flow / dialogue / oracle / return

Deep M4′
    full protected psychoid instrument
    body / chakra / quaternion / resonance / trajectory /
    symbolic integration / identity evidence / cymatic dynamics
```

## 2. The six deep products are native instruments, not detail pages

Each M′ domain may become a bespoke field of composition, interaction and technique using the native substrate that actually belongs to it.

Opening depth means **entering the same phenomenon at its own scale**.

It does not mean opening an administrative page describing that phenomenon.

A deep instrument may therefore use a different technical body from the parent composition:

- graph/world rendering;
- Bevy/wgpu/WebGPU;
- dense particle/cymatic simulation;
- audio/MIDI/MPE surfaces;
- specialised document or symbolic editor;
- native code/knowledge workbench;
- agentic/pedagogical composition;
- alternate local or remote native surface.

The shared requirement is semantic continuity: the deep instrument opens from the same event/subject/coordinate relation and preserves provenance back to its source.

---

## 3. The 0/5 boundary-expression law

A repeated structure in the per-M′ architecture becomes visible when the six domains are read together:

- `.0` repeatedly carries ground, source, invariant, identity or reception;
- `.5` repeatedly carries totalisation, topology, rendering, integration, return or the seam into the next relation;
- `.1`–`.4` carry the inner differentiated dynamics.

This document makes the following **source-grounded product proposition** explicit:

> **For each deep M′ product, the `.0/.5` framing pair defines how that domain can remain minimally and truthfully present in a parent 3:3 composition. The inner `.1–.4` field remains selectively summonable in the parent and becomes fully compositional in the deep product.**

This is not an instruction to hide `.1–.4` from the parent. Nara journalling is naturally M4-4-heavy; oracle activity naturally crosses M4-2; transformation/flow may cross M4-3. The point is different:

```text
M#.0  tells the parent what must remain grounded / authoritative
M#.5  tells the parent what the domain resolves or returns as
M#.1-.4 supply task-specific capabilities as needed

DEEP M#′
  composes the whole 4+2 field as its own native instrument
```

This prevents the parent product from accidentally becoming a permanently expanded deep instrument while preserving access to genuine internal dynamics.

The exact minimal projection is domain-specific and must be authored/tested per M′. The law is a design constraint, not a substitute for those specifications.

## 5. Parent and deep are the same world at different scale

The parent/deep relation should preserve:

```text
same current event / subject
same canonical coordinate refs
same source/provider lineage
same privacy and authority law
same Actions where the act is semantically the same
```

while allowing:

```text
different camera / scale
different native renderer
different interaction density
different explanatory depth
different number of simultaneously active internal dynamics
```

This yields the central experiential principle:

> **Depth changes scale and composition, not ontology.**

A person should be able to select a current planet/correspondence in Cosmic and open deep M2′; select the current clock/signature and open deep M3′; open the current Nara state into deep M4′; enter a Bimba relation into deep M0′ — without a second semantic object being minted merely because the UI became richer.

## 6. Relation to the O:I desktop

Epi does not need a separate shell in order to remain a distinctive application.

The O:I desktop gives Epi ordinary application primitives — Canvas, tabs, splits, Navigator/Knowledge, Agent sidecar, Inspector, Command, Lower/System regions, explicit Explore projection — while Epi retains ownership of its resources, Actions, readings and native bodies.

This is especially important for the eightfold:

- the two composed products can occupy primary Canvas Surfaces;
- each deep M′ instrument can open as an ordinary current/new tab or split;
- Nara daily writing can use native document/canvas primitives;
- Epii conversation can remain the canonical AgentSession in the sidecar;
- Bimba/source orientation can use shared Knowledge/navigation services;
- specialist GPU/audio/graph bodies remain native instead of being flattened into React;
- presentation arrangement is not semantic identity.

The desktop is therefore the **composable host of the eightfold**, not the ontology that defines it.

## 7. Social and shared-world horizon

The source corpus already differentiates M0 as canonical world-ground and M5 as pedagogy/return. The wider O:I programme adds explicit SharedField/Explore relations.

A future inhabited Epi world may therefore let Bimba become spatially traversable, with Epi agents and explicitly projected participants inhabiting or encountering that world. The important ownership distinction remains:

```text
M0 / Bimba
    canonical structural world-ground

M5 / Epii
    inhabitation, traversal, disclosure, pedagogy, social/recognition relation

O:I SharedField / Explore
    explicit encounter and Projection between independently grounded worlds
```

Protected Nara state never becomes public merely because it is locally addressable. Any avatar or shared representation is an explicit projection with its own authority and provenance.

## 8. Epistemic status of this document

### Recovered source commitments

- 3:3 Cosmic and Personal composition surfaces;
- recursive sixfold / 4+2 internal M′ depth;
- composition over juxtaposition;
- one current profile/event relation across projections;
- strong per-M′ native render/instrument designs;
- recurring `.0/.5` frame across the six M′ architectures;
- protected Nara psychoid/quaternionic/chakra field;
- M2 solar-system spatial anchor and live harmonic/correspondential field;
- M3 360°/720° clock and symbolic-genetic inscription;
- M0 Bimba world/graph ground;
- M5 pedagogical/agentic return.

### New authored design commitments

- treat the application as an **eightfold product field: two composed products + six deep instruments**;
- reserve the complete 4+2 dynamics of an M′ domain primarily for its deep product rather than making the parent composition permanently deep;
- define parent→deep as a scale/composition transition over the same semantic event;
- treat the `.0/.5` frame as the boundary-expression grammar through which a deep M′ remains minimally truthful in 3:3 composition.

### New design propositions still requiring returned reality

- the exact minimal `.0/.5` projection for every domain;
- final parent-scale spatial grammar for Cosmic and Personal;
- which deep instruments require native GPU/audio/graph bodies in the first implementation tranche;
- how much of the future inhabited/social Epi world belongs locally before explicit Explore projection.

The project should let implementation and experience revise these propositions explicitly rather than silently turning one provisional renderer into permanent doctrine.
