# Canonical Intent

## Product Identity

[[M']] is the coded experiential expression of the system. It consumes [[S]] and [[S']] services; it does not become a second owner of kernel, graph, session, agent, review, or canon law. The product is one continuous operational environment in which the same coordinate, profile generation, session, DAY/NOW context, and evidence lineage remain visible across work.

Primary authority: [[M'-SYSTEM-SPEC]] (lines 103-194) and [[M'-PORTAL-SPEC]] (lines 80-224).

The binding carrier decision is the Tauri v2 app at `Body/M/pratibimba-app/`. The older Theia material is a frozen parts warehouse and design genealogy. This is explicit in [[M'-SYSTEM-SPEC]] (lines 551-635), [[M5'-SPEC]] (lines 203-211), and `Body/M/AGENTS.md` (lines 4-12).

## Required Product Grammar

The canonical product has three layers that must not collapse into one another.

| Layer | Purpose | Required experience |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | Cosmic/structural parent face | Lean daily orientation through the integrated [[M1']]/[[M2']]/[[M3']] instrument. It is a preview and point of entry, not thirteen independent subsystem tabs. |
| `/` | Transverse operator membrane | Commands, conversation, sessions, tools, readiness, evidence, review, diagnostics, configuration, and in-flight agent activity. It crosses all work without becoming a second application. |
| `1` | Personal/world-return parent face | Lean daily orientation through [[M4']] continuity, [[M5']] review/pedagogy, and renewed [[M0']] ground. It is not the full [[M4']]/[[M5']] workbench. |
| [[M0']]-[[M5']] | Six deep subsystem pages | Full workspaces with their own inner 0-5 strata, reached from Home, commands, or the membrane while retaining shared state. |

Home defaults to the 0/1 parent experience and can reveal the six subsystem entries. The shell and deep pages coexist. They are not duplicate tab collections ([[M'-SYSTEM-SPEC]], lines 105-122).

## Continuous State Spine

Every transition must preserve:

- selected coordinate and pointer anchors;
- profile generation, tick, lens, mode, and codon projection;
- session, DAY/NOW, privacy class, and deposition context;
- active files/artifacts and unsaved work;
- in-flight agent task, tool, evidence, and review lineage;
- service readiness and degraded-state reason.

The shell faces are the same state seen through different registers. They must not feel like unrelated applications ([[M'-SYSTEM-SPEC]], lines 187-203).

## Coordinate Workspaces

### [[M0']] - Bimba Map And Verification

[[M0']] is the full graph/map explorer: coordinate search, graph traversal, source/spec/code/test anchors, readiness, and routes into the other domains. Technical relation metadata is summonable; the default is a readable graph, not a provenance dump ([[M0'-SPEC]], lines 43-89).

### [[M1']] - Relation Walk And Played Topology

[[M1']] makes relation movement playable: coordinate walk, torus/path traversal, profile-driven sound, and the 84-state lens/mode landscape. It consumes graph and profile authority and never invents pitch or time ([[M1'-SPEC]], lines 34-137).

### [[M2']] - Correspondence And Cymatic Meaning

[[M2']] is the 72-fold harmonic-correspondential instrument: lens matrix, element and planetary/chakral panels, cymatic surface, Klein flip, and meaning-packet inspector. It exposes live backend meaning, not local folklore tables ([[M2'-SPEC]], lines 33-158).

### [[M3']] - Clock, Cosmos, And Transcription

[[M3']] renders the shared harmonic clock, 64/472 codon-rotation landscape, 72-to-64 transduction evidence, and double-torus world-clock. It must not fork clock, graph, codon, Tarot, or physics authority ([[M3'-SPEC]], lines 39-183).

### [[M4']] - Protected Personal Continuity

[[M4']] owns the protected lived field: Daily Note, journal, dream, oracle, highlights, session close, personal resonance, and temporal trajectory. Its default is dialogical and lived, not a quaternion or identity dump. It is the strictest privacy boundary ([[M4'-SPEC]], lines 49-101 and 321-417).

### [[M5']] - Agent-Led IDE And Review Workbench

[[M5']] is the deep agent-led developer and pedagogical IDE. Conversation is primary. Library, Canon Studio, Backend Studio, Frontend Studio, Agentic Control Room, and Logos Atelier are summoned contexts around active work. It must expose task/run/tool/evidence/review lineage and enforce [[S5]] governance ([[M5'-SPEC]], lines 39-163).

The sixfold [[M5']] workbench is not six more explanatory pages. It is where the user opens files, asks and directs agents, inspects tools, reviews diffs and evidence, edits governed artifacts, and decides promotion.

## Canonical UX Laws

1. Conversational and operational default; technical detail on request.
2. One shell, one profile stream, one session spine, one command system.
3. Parent shell previews; subsystem pages deliver depth.
4. Coordinates orient and route work; they are not a justification for one tab per concept.
5. Missing substrate is named and actionable, but it does not dominate the main work surface.
6. Private bodies remain protected-local; public surfaces show only governed handles and summaries.
7. Review and promotion are explicit human workflows, never implied by agent completion.
