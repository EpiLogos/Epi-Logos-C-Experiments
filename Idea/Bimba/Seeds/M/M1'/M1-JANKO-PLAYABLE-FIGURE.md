## 0. Figure relation

The Jankó keyboard is useful to M1′ because its actual keyboard geometry gives a concrete playable surface on which two relations already native to the QL musical derivation can be apprehended together:

```text
First Spanda   3 : 3
Second Spanda  4 : 2
```

The historical instrument is not a source of QL and does not validate the Spanda derivation. It is a **Figure**: an existing instrument morphology that makes an already-authored formal relation unusually tangible.

---

## 1. Historical/instrumental evidence

Paul von Jankó's keyboard was designed in the 1880s as a multi-row isomorphic alternative to the ordinary piano keyboard.

Stable construction facts relevant here:

- six rows in the original/full arrangement;
- two interleaved whole-tone row families;
- rows 1/3/5 repeat one whole-tone set and rows 2/4/6 repeat the semitone-offset set;
- every pitch therefore has three touch-points on a six-row instrument;
- neighbouring columns / alternate-row neighbours are a semitone apart, while adjacent notes within a row are a whole tone apart;
- the traditional natural/accidental colouring can be retained.

Historical/reference evidence:

- Smithsonian Institution, *Piano 300: Celebrating Three Centuries of People and Pianos* collection text: six ranks, first/third/fifth rows on one whole-tone set, alternate rows on the semitone-offset set.  
  https://repository.si.edu/bitstreams/76ab52f7-b896-4298-b070-7b7589bf32bc/download
- General construction summary: two interleaved manuals, three touch-points per lever, six rows; rows move by whole tone and adjacent alternate rows by semitone.  
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jank%C3%B3_keyboard
- Daskin Uniform Keyboard Systems, Jankó layout description: traditional colouring yields four white/two black on one repeated row-family and three white/three black on the alternating family.  
  https://daskin.com/page5/page5.html

The colour ratio is especially useful as a Figure because the same six-note row can be read through two visible partitions:

```text
row-family A: 4 white : 2 black
row-family B: 3 white : 3 black
```

The two counts are not imposed by QL; they arise from retaining ordinary C-major natural/accidental colouring on the Jankó whole-tone row geometry.

## 2. QL reading of the Figure

The project-level relation is:

```text
one sixfold playable surface
        ↓
concomitant partition readings
        ├─ 3 : 3
        └─ 4 : 2
        ↓
whole-tone horizontal movement
        ↕
semitone diagonal movement
        ↓
12-position chromatic completion
```

This is significant because the QL musical derivation itself moves from the First/Second Spanda partitions into exact ratios and then into two co-foundational twelve-position bases. Jankó supplies an instrument morphology in which **sixfold partition, two whole-tone helices and chromatic interleaving are already a single tactile geometry**.

The Figure therefore makes a latent relation perceptible:

> the 3:3 and 4:2 are not competing ways to divide six; they can be two simultaneously available readings of one playable sixfold whose interleaving generates access to the twelvefold chromatic field.

This sentence is a current Epi/QL interpretive proposition. Its derivation remains answerable to the QL source; the keyboard provides embodied intelligibility, not formal authority.

## 3. Relation to A/B/C and D1→D3

The keyboard Figure should consume the canonical harmonic grammar rather than invent another relation system.

```text
QL position field
    ↓
A / B / C relation-family selection
    ↓
D1 → D2 → D3 conjugate completion
    ↓
exact interval / traversal basis
    ↓
Jankó spatial rendering + sound
```

A/B/C must remain visibly distinguishable from conjugate-completion degree. A useful M1′ interaction therefore highlights:

- the selected direct positional dyad;
- its A/B/C family;
- direct vs prime face;
- any newly opened conjugate coordinate as D1 expands through D2 to D3;
- the exact rational interval realised in the current traversal basis;
- the repeated equivalent touch-points for the same pitch.

The surface is thereby a **relation instrument**, not merely an alternative MIDI keyboard.

---

## 4. Relation to the two traversal bases

Jankó naturally foregrounds the chromatic/whole-tone basis:

```text
horizontal row       = whole-tone advance
alternate-row move   = semitone offset
2 interleaved rows   = 12 pitch classes
3 repeated row-pairs = ergonomic/isomorphic redundancy
```

The fifths basis remains co-foundational in QL and should be renderable over the same pitch field as an alternate traversal overlay. The keyboard geometry does not replace the fifth-cycle; it gives one particularly direct body to the chromatic/two-helix basis while the instrument can highlight fifth-relations across that same twelve-position substrate.

A native M1′ Jankó mode should be capable of presenting all of the following in one current state:

```text
six rows / repeated touch-points
3:3 partition overlay
4:2 colour/partition overlay
whole-tone row continuity
semitone diagonals
current tick12 / direct-prime phase
A/B/C relation family
D1/D2/D3 conjugate completion
```

The player should be able to strike the same formal relation from more than one equivalent touch-point and receive the same kernel identity/provenance. That is an executable test of isomorphism within the M1′ instrument.

## 6. Acceptance

The Figure has been integrated properly when:

1. six-row geometry is generated from the twelve-position musical substrate rather than drawn as an unrelated keyboard skin;
2. pitch-equivalent repeated touch-points resolve to one canonical musical/kernel address;
3. horizontal whole-tone and diagonal semitone relations are explicit and testable;
4. `3:3` and `4:2` overlays can coexist without changing the underlying pitch identity;
5. A/B/C family and D1→D3 completion are independently inspectable;
6. exact rational interval and provenance are returned for each relation;
7. chromatic and fifths traversals remain two bases of the same pitch field;
8. historical Jankó evidence remains marked as Figure evidence, distinct from authored QL derivation and implementation evidence.
