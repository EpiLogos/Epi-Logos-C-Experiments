# 12.T12.9 Moirai Night-Pass Routing Audit

## Finding

The `s4'.mediation.route` handling of `dispatch_moirai_night_pass` is live and
chains into the Anima and Aletheia implementations without a carrier gap.

- `Body/S/S0/epi-cli/src/gate/anima.rs` declares the tool, classifies it as
  `MoiraiNightPass`, requires `CFP3`, `Night'`, and an allowed host CF, and
  applies the `aletheia-mode-internal` entitlement class.
- `Body/S/S4/ta-onta/S4-4p-anima/modules/moirai-dispatch.ts` constructs the
  Night-pass fan-out at CFP3 for Klotho (P1), Lachesis (P4), and Atropos (P5).
- `Body/S/S4/ta-onta/S4-5p-aletheia/modules/moirai-rehear.ts` requires the
  complete three-position gather, records the re-hearing, and returns the Epii
  handoff target. It rejects incomplete input rather than treating a partial
  gather as a completed pass.
