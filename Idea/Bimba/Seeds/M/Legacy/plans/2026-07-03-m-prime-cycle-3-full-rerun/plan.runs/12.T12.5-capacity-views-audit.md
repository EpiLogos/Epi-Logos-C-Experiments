# 12.T12.5 Capacity Views Audit

## Disposition

Retain the six operational capacities as one governed [[M5']] autoresearch monitoring
surface in the active [[Pratibimba]] carrier. Do not restore them as separate
[[OmniPanel]] tabs or revive the frozen [[Theia]] widgets.

## Evidence

- `Body/S/S5/epii-autoresearch-core/src/capacity_workflows/registry.rs` owns six
  executable profiles: Anuttara, Paramasiva, Parashakti, Mahamaya, Nara, and
  Epii-on-Epii. Their routes carry real candidate, review, governance, and
  promotion data through the [[S5]] stores.
- `Body/M/pratibimba-app/src/panes/AutoresearchPane.tsx` mounts six selectable
  capacity controls over live `s5'.improve.*` records. It filters candidates and
  shows candidate and human-gate counts; it does not create a second command or
  tab surface.
- `Body/M/pratibimba-app/src/panes/omni/omnipanelRuntime.ts` explicitly excludes
  the six capacities from the eight [[OmniPanel]] tabs, preserving DR-TS-4.

## Verification

- `cargo test --offline --manifest-path Body/S/S5/epii-autoresearch-core/Cargo.toml --test capacity_workflow_registry`
  passed 2/2: every profile routes through real autoresearch/review stores,
  reloads into a six-panel snapshot, and rejects a fabricated review route.
- `pnpm playwright test tests/e2e/autoresearch.spec.ts` from
  `Body/M/pratibimba-app` passed 3/3 against real Chromium, a spawned gateway,
  and the filesystem-backed vault sidecar. The mounted Autoresearch surface
  exposes seven filter options and six capacity buttons, then completes governed
  review and pair-composition flows.
