## PART B — WHAT THE REAL SCIENCE SAYS (hard vs speculative)

### B.1 The conserved-delta anchor is real and non-allometric **[HARD]**

- **Animal acoustic communication converges on ~2.7 Hz across 98 species** (58 birds, 28 mammals, 4
  amphibians, 4 insects, 1 reptile, 1 fish); optimal rhythm **2.7 Hz, 95% CI [0.45, 4.99] Hz**, matching
  the neural **delta range (1–4 Hz)**; explicitly **non-allometric** — "rhythm evolution cannot be explained
  by weight, environmental pressures, or social complexity." The authors root it in **conserved brain
  oscillations**. *(De Gregorio et al., PLOS Biology, June 2026, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003798.)*
- **Brain-rhythm frequency bands are evolutionarily preserved across ~a 17,000-fold brain-volume range**
  in mammals — oscillatory classes and their frequencies conserved despite anatomical scaling.
  *(Buzsáki, Logothetis & Singer, "Scaling brain size, keeping timing," Neuron 2013, PMC4009705; the
  log-scaled band hierarchy is Buzsáki & Mizuseki, "The log-dynamic brain," Nat. Rev. Neurosci. 2014.)*
  **Citation note:** the "Buzsáki & Mizuseki 2013" handle in the plan conflates two papers — the
  *non-allometric brain-size* result is **Buzsáki, Logothetis & Singer 2013 (Neuron)**; **Mizuseki**
  co-authors the 2014 band-hierarchy review. Cite precisely.

**Nuance to keep honest [HARD]:** the PLOS 2026 paper attributes the conserved rhythm to **conserved neural
time constants / brain oscillations**, and does **not** itself invoke coupled oscillators or CPGs as the
mechanism. So the delta finding gives a **frequency band**, not a coordination model.

### B.2 HKB is real — but it is a behavioral/neural *coordination* model, not the delta paper's mechanism **[HARD]**

The Haken–Kelso–Bunz model (Haken, Kelso & Bunz, *Biological Cybernetics* 1985; Scholarpedia "Haken-Kelso-Bunz
model") reduces two coupled nonlinear oscillators to a single relative-phase equation with **bistability
(in-phase φ=0 and antiphase φ=π coexist at low frequency) and a bifurcation to monostability (only in-phase)
as movement frequency / coupling rises.** It is extensively validated for **bimanual and interpersonal motor
coordination** — i.e. movements in the ~1–4 Hz range. It is a genuine, canonical model.

**The bridge the architecture builds [SPECULATIVE — a synthesis, not a sourced claim]:** No paper states
"the conserved 2.7 Hz delta *is* an HKB relative-phase field." HKB (a coordination law) and the delta
conservation (an evolutionary-neuroscience finding) live in the **same frequency regime** (delta-ish), so
pairing them is *plausible and regime-consistent* — but it is the Epi-Logos synthesis, not established
science. Label it as a modeling choice.

### B.3 The scale gap — the clean negative (this is the load-bearing finding) **[HARD]**

- The tick's delta anchor is **~2.5–2.7 Hz** → period ~0.4 s → a **neural/behavioral** rhythm.
- The actual molecular physics of a codon — base-pair opening, phonon-like collective modes that gate
  transcription/replication/denaturation — is **terahertz**: distinct DNA/base features in **0.5–3 THz**,
  sequence-distinguishing modes **< 1 THz**, and a characteristic DNA-unwinding drive at **44 THz**.
  *(Markelz, "THz spectroscopy of biological molecules," J. Biol. Phys. 2012, PubMed 23345823; González
  Jiménez et al., "coherent delocalized phonon-like modes in DNA," 2016, PMC4895446; Wu et al., "Terahertz
  wave accelerates DNA unwinding," J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2020.)*
- **Gap: ~12 orders of magnitude (10⁰ Hz vs 10¹² Hz).** There is **no known physical mechanism** — no
  resonance ladder, no harmonic bridge — coupling a 2.5 Hz delta rhythm to codon-scale molecular vibration.

**Conclusion [HARD]:** the tick **cannot** honestly be claimed to be the codon's physical frequency, to
physically drive codon chemistry, or to resonate with base-pair dynamics. Any tick↔codon relation in this
system is **architectural/symbolic** (a shared discrete clock index; a symbolic M2→M3 address transform) —
which is fine, provided it is never dressed as physics.

### B.4 Standing-wave / Chladni mode structure — no privileged 12-fold or 64-fold basis **[HARD]**

Chladni/plate and circular-membrane eigenmodes form a **two-parameter family** — indexed by nodal diameters
`m` and nodal circles `n` (Bessel `J_m` for the disk); a `cos(mθ)` mode shows `m` nodal diameters. Counting
and classifying modes is generally hard except for separable or fully-chaotic plates. *(COMSOL, "How do
Chladni plates visualize sound"; Tseng et al., "Resonant modes for circular and polygonal Chladni plates,"
2024, PMC10969725.)* **There is no natural "12-mode" or "64-mode" standing-wave structure** — you can *find*
a mode whose pattern reads as 12-fold (6 nodal diameters) or as any other symmetry, but it is one arbitrary
member of an infinite spectrum, not a canonical basis. **So neither the twelvefold nor the 64-fold is
grounded in standing-wave physics; both are combinatorial/number-theoretic in this system** (12 = spanda
periodicity / chromatic ring; 64 = 4³ = 2⁶ codon address space).

### B.7 Cross-scale synchronization — real *within* the neural regime, not across to molecular **[HARD]**

Phase-locking, `n:m` synchronization, **Arnold tongues**, and cross-frequency (e.g. theta–gamma
