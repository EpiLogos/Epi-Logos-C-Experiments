# 15.T15.8 Ananda Vortex Carrier Audit

## Result: Partial Carrier, No Honest Full-Fidelity Close

`Body/M/pratibimba-app` has a real K2 played-torus carrier, but the present
profile projection cannot support the full T15.8 visual contract without the
frontend reconstructing kernel-owned data.

## Landed Carrier Behavior

- `src/panes/playedTorusScene.ts` renders one K2 with its SU(2) Hopf shadow,
  applies the bus-provided ring quaternion, places the bus-provided active cell
  on the torus surface, and colors the halo from the bus-provided Cl(4,2)
  signature.
- `src/panes/m1PlayedTorus.ts` is read-only: it accepts only a strict
  `anandaVortex` projection and renders an explicit pending state when absent.
- The active cell retains both raw and digit-root faces as supplied by the
  profile bus. It does not locally derive values.

## Missing Contract Data

The T15.8 contract requires six stacked 12x12 dual-register heatmaps and two
ordered DR streamlines. The current `AnandaVortexProjection` supplies only:

- one `activeMatrixOp` and one fully populated `activeCellValue`;
- the active cell address;
- `drRingPhase` values, not an ordered six-position streamline payload; and
- orientation, sheet, and Cl(4,2) fields.

It does not carry the remaining 143 cells of the active family, the other five
families, or an ordered backend-owned representation of
`DR_RING_MAHAMAYA` / `DR_RING_PARASHAKTI`. Creating a client-side matrix or
value-to-index mapping would violate the canonical reads-only rule and fork
kernel law into the carrier.

## Evidence

- `pnpm vitest run src/panes/playedTorusScene.test.ts src/panes/m1PlayedTorus.test.ts`
  passed: 19 tests. These exercise K2 topology, bus quaternion application,
  bus-addressed active-cell placement, pending behavior, and the Cl(4,2)
  color binary.
- Source inspection: `portal-core/src/kernel/projections/ananda_vortex.rs`
  defines the current one-cell `AnandaVortexProjection`; it contains no matrix
  collection or ordered streamline geometry/indices.
- Canon: `[[M1-2-ANANDA-VORTEX-ARCHITECTURE]]` section 5 requires six
  12x12 dual-register layers and backend-owned DR streamline data; section 9
  forbids a local TypeScript LUT fork.

## Required Follow-up

Before the full visual layer can land, the S0 projection contract needs a
backend-owned, serialized matrix/streamline render payload (or an equally
authoritative backend render artifact). That is a contract-surface change and
must be owned and canon-flagged by the relevant S0 / M1-2 architecture work;
this carrier tranche must not invent the missing data.

External review remains Claude-owned. This audit is not a verification or
done-closure receipt.
