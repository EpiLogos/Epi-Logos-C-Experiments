## Standing invariants honored

- **Composition over juxtaposition (15-foundation principle 6, 15.4 contract).** Integrated plugins compose three M-extensions into ONE coherent editor surface — never three side-by-side widgets. Three-pane juxtaposition is the antipattern. The composition coordinator (`composition-coordinator.ts:73`) resolves contributor claims onto SHARED slots (center-stage, side-panel, audio-bus, selection-owner, evidence-panel) with conflict surfacing; Wave-C extends the slot taxonomy to `surface | texture | cell-state | grounding` for geometric composition.
- **Profile-tick as single render clock (15-foundation principle 2, 15.6).** Both compositions subscribe to `MathemeHarmonicProfile` tick-advance through a SINGLE `ProfileTickSubscription` per composition — not three subscriptions from three contributors. The shared subscription fans out to all three poles. Cross-link 22.10 dispatch contract (M1 standalone-vs-composed) + 23.10 cymatic compositionMountPoint reads-only contract + 24.13 M3 codon-rotation composition export.
- **Reads-only contract.** Composition NEVER mutates substrate. M2 cymatic engine renders frequencies ON the K² torus surface (per 15.4 + M2-ARCHITECTURE §7.6 "the cymatic IS the surface texture of the played torus"); M2 owns the data, M1 owns the surface, M3 codon-rotation overlays the lens-ring cells. Cross-link 23.10 reads-only contract + 24.13 reads-only export.
- **M4 protected-local privacy class throughout 4-5-0 paths.** Composition contract (08-t0) §"Shared Rules" — M4 protected-local body never crosses the public bridge; recognition surfaces consume Graphiti protected handles only. `composition-coordinator.ts:204-227` `enforceProtectedLocalBoundary()` is the existing enforcement; Wave-C extends it for the geometric composition slots.
- **SharedBridgeAdapter only.** Composition contract §"Shared Rules" — no integrated plugin imports raw `Body/S/S0`, `Body/S/S2`, `Body/S/S3`, `Body/S/S5`, `@clockworklabs/spacetimedb-sdk`, `neo4j-driver`, `redis`, `portal-core`, or `epii-review-core`. Every cross-stack call is via `SharedBridgeAdapter`. Existing `bridge-gate.ts` (browser) is the bridge-availability gate; Wave-C extends with composition-specific readiness gating.
- **Honor 15-foundation principles 1-9.** Coordinate as primary navigation; profile-tick as primary clock; provenance always visible; Bimba/Pratibimba as UI dial; OmniPanel as `/` operator membrane; composition over juxtaposition; activity-bar discipline; Theia conventions; day-now as ambient thread.
- **137 = 64 + 72 + 1 visual matheme.** Cosmic engine displays the spine: M3 codon (64) + M2 invariant (72) + M1-5 (+1) = 137 (per Track 07 §7.2 + 19.8 Kaprekar + 19.9 7-8-9 spine). Composition surfaces this visually, not just numerically.
- **Two roles, one substrate.** Standalone deep widgets (per Tracks 22-26) AND composed contributions share the SAME React component when rendered. Mode is a discriminator on the contribution, not a duplicate body.
