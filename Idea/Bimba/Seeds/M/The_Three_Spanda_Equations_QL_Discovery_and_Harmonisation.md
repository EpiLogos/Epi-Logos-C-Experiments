$$1_{pole}\quad\quad\text{and}\quad\quad 1_{actual}.$$

The first is the affirmative pole internal to the primal distinction. The second is the invariant achieved when both orientations of the distinction have completed their mutual passage:

$$1_{actual} = 1/1 = 100\%.$$
