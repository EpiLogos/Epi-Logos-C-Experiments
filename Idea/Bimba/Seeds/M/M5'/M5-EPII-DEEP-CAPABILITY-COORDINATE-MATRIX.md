Strong 1:1 correspondences are recorded where the source actually establishes them; they are not used to flatten the wider field.

## 1. Recovered subsystem meaning

Epii is the **holographic self-articulation, recognition and return faculty** of Epi-Logos. Its distinguishing concern is not another object-domain alongside M0–M4, but the system becoming available to itself across identity, reason, technical embodiment, experiential/deep composition, operative activity, cognition and return.

The original canonical M5 source states the outer relation directly:

```text
M5.0  what the system IS
M5.1  WHY it exists
M5.2 / M5.3  HOW it is materially / experientially realised
M5.4  WHO / WHAT actively operates it
M5.5  the protocol / method of articulation-recognition-return
```

The current fourfold correction changes how some old implementation-family identifications are read, but not the deeper capability field. In the current Epi/QL projection the strongest working reading is:

```text
M5.0  system identity theoretics / holographic self-model
M5.1  philosophical self-articulation / canon
M5.2  standing technological / product self-reflection (S field)
M5.3  deep compositional / experiential self-reflection (M′ field)
M5.4  operative activity / agency self-reflection (S′ activity + Guardian/Agency relations)
M5.5  Logos / articulation-recognition-return
```

This is a **relational matrix**, not six independent applications and not a mandatory six-step workflow.

### Boundary relation

`M5` is the implicate return pole of the sixfold. `M5 -> M0` is not merely navigation to the next item. It is the recognition/return relation by which differentiated experience, knowledge and work can revise the articulated ground **through explicit authority and provenance**, rather than by silently mutating source.
