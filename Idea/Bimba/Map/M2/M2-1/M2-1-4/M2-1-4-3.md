# M2-1-4-3 · Psychology (mediating structure)

> Interpretive knowing - the knower as part of the known

## Detail
- **Description:** Psychology here is phenomenological - studying consciousness as intentional structure not as natural object. This is Husserl's pure psychology or transcendental phenomenology - revealing consciousness as world-constituting. The third stage where consciousness recognizes itself as mediator between multiplicity (givenness) and unity (meaning). The narrative self emerges - identity constructed through temporal continuity (childhood → adolescence → adulthood). CRITICAL LIMITATION: Psychology studies time from outside - as object, not yet recognizing itself AS historical being.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as third explicate epistemic position - investigating knowing subject through personal biographical time, still treating history as individual rather than recognizing being AS historical. Completes (2-3) dyadic core, reaching limit that drives Dasein turn.
- **Operational symbolics:** Autobiography (personal story); mirror (self-reflection); timeline of life (biographical); photograph album (personal memory); growth rings (development)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as biographical investigation: Psychology examines the knowing subject through personal history. The self as narrative - story told across time giving identity coherence. Developmental stages (Freud, Erikson, Piaget) as individual temporal structures. Memory, habituation, character formation as personal-temporal phenomena. But still treating time as framework FOR individual rather than recognizing individual AS temporal-historical.
- **Consciousness structure:** Self-reflexive biographical consciousness; awareness of personal history; the narrative self examining itself
- **Key principles:** Third-Order Knowing: Investigating the knowing subject; Phenomenological Psychology: Consciousness as intentional structure; Transcendental Ego: Husserl's constituting consciousness; Lived Body: Merleau-Ponty's corps vécu - experiencing embodiment; Biographical Time: Personal history and individual development; Narrative Self: Identity through temporal continuity; Mediating Structure: Neither pure subject nor object
- **Architectural function:** Third explicate position - mediating stage where subject-object split is problematized. Psychology asks "Who knows?" - revealing knower is not detached observer but embodied participant. This is stage recognizing knowledge is always someone's knowledge - not view from nowhere but situated perspective.
