# M2-1-4-1 · Ontology (what/being)

> Foundational knowing - establishing what IS

## Detail
- **Description:** Ontology is pre-theoretical but explicit - we know things exist without yet asking how we know. This is the natural attitude Husserl describes - naively believing in world's existence before phenomenological reflection. The first stage where consciousness becomes directional (intentional) - consciousness OF something. Still relatively ahistorical - being grasped in its thisness without temporal depth. The synchronic slice emphasizing present being.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as first explicate epistemic position - explicit recognition of being emphasizing present existence. Forms actual pole in phenomenological [0↔1] oscillation.
- **Operational symbolics:** Present moment (now); vertical axis (synchronic); spotlight (illuminating present); snapshot (captured instant); here-now (spatial-temporal presence)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as present-oriented recognition: Ontology emphasizes what IS (present tense) - the being presenting itself now. Though phenomenologically the "now" contains retentional-protentional horizon, ontology focuses on presence. First differentiation from pre-reflective flow - singling out entities from background. The "what" as answer to being-question, emphasizing thisness over process.
- **Consciousness structure:** Present-oriented consciousness; awareness of what-is-now; the synchronic grasp of being
- **Key principles:** Ontological Recognition: Awareness that things ARE; Synchronic Presence: Emphasis on the now (though never punctual); Perspectival Knowing: Consciousness takes viewpoint; Pre-Ontological Understanding: Heidegger's primordial grasp of being; Natural Attitude: Naive belief in world's existence (Husserl); Intentional Consciousness: Directional awareness-of; Ahistorical Focus: Being-as-present without temporal depth yet
- **Architectural function:** First explicate position - the initiation of reflective knowing. Ontology asks "What is?" - the most basic epistemic question. This is perspectival knowing - consciousness takes point of view on world. Following Merleau-Ponty, perception is already interpretation - we don't perceive neutral data then add meaning; perception gives meaningful wholes. Temporal emphasis: synchronic (the present moment), though phenomenologically this "now" is never punctual but specious present.
- **Practical applications:** Ontological Inquiry: Asking "what is?" foundationally; Presence Practice: Attending to what is present now; Perceptual Training: Recognizing how perception gives meaning; Being-Awareness: Cultivating wonder at sheer existence; Synchronic Analysis: Examining present structure; Naive Phenomenology: Starting with natural attitude; Entity Recognition: Distinguishing beings from background
- **Resonances:** Heidegger: Pre-ontological understanding, being vs. beings; Husserl: Transition passive-to-active synthesis; Merleau-Ponty: Perception as primordial access to being; Parmenides: "What is, is" - ontological affirmation; Presence Metaphysics: Traditional emphasis on present being; Synchronic Analysis: Structural linguistics' present-focus; Archetypal One: "What" questions answering essence
