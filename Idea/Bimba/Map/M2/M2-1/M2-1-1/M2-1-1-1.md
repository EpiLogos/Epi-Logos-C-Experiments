# M2-1-1-1 · Material cause (what)

> The substantial ground - raw sensory data and concrete facts that form reality's material basis

## Detail
- **Description:** Material cause is passive potency requiring efficient cause's active potency to manifest. But this passivity is rich - like soil containing nutrients, the material substrate constrains and enables what forms can emerge. Not dead matter but living material - Aristotle's hyle as potential awaiting actualization. Jung's sensation function operates here: direct, non-interpretive contact with what-is-given. The first explicate dimension of causal quaternary.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as first explicate causal position providing material foundation that subsequent positions (efficient, formal, final) actualize and direct. Forms actual pole in causal [0↔1] oscillation.
- **Operational symbolics:** Clay (shapeable substance); bronze (statue material); soil (plant substrate); flesh (embodied existence); raw data (informational substrate)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as substrate-potency: Material cause is what something is made from (bronze for statue, cells for organism, sensory data for perception). Provides continuity through change - the matter persists even as form changes. Contains implicit potency for multiple actualizations - bronze could become statue OR sword OR coin. The "what" as answer to compositional analysis.
- **Consciousness structure:** Sensory-grounded consciousness; awareness anchored in concrete materiality; the "is-ness" of phenomena
- **Key principles:** Hyle (Matter): The stuff/substrate from which things are made; Passive Potency: Capacity to receive form and actualization; Jungian Sensation: Direct sensory contact with concrete givenness; Compositional Analysis: Answering "what is it made of?"; Material Continuity: Substrate persisting through formal changes; Rich Potency: Matter constrains and enables possible forms; Facticity: Brute givenness of material presence
- **Architectural function:** First explicate position in causal quaternary - the substrate upon which all subsequent causes operate. Material cause provides the "that which" can be shaped (formal), energized (efficient), and directed (final). Jung's sensation grounds consciousness in concrete actuality - touching, tasting, seeing the facticity of existence. Mirrors Level 0's archetypal one - first differentiation from void.
- **Practical applications:** Compositional Analysis: Understanding what things are made of; Sensory Grounding: Returning to direct sensory experience; Resource Identification: Recognizing available materials; Phenomenological Description: Attending to concrete givenness; Ergonomics: Working with material properties and constraints; Nutritional Science: Body as requiring material substrates; Art/Craft: Understanding medium's material properties
- **Resonances:** Aristotle: Hyle as potency (dynamis) awaiting form; Jung: Sensation function - concrete, factual, present-oriented; Whitehead: Physical feelings as conformal reception of past; Four Elements: Earth as most material (heaviness, solidity); Phenomenology: The given-as-given before interpretation; Buddhism: Rupa (form/materiality) as first skandha; Bronze Statue: Classic example - bronze is material cause of Apollo statue
