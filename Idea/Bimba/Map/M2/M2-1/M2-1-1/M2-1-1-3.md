# M2-1-1-3 · Formal cause (who/mediating structure)

> The structuring intelligence - mental patterns that give intelligible form to experience

## Detail
- **Description:** Formal cause is structural principle - the blueprint, template, essence. Not static (as medieval scholastics sometimes portrayed) but dynamic pattern - like Whiteheadian "eternal objects" ingressing into actual occasions, or Platonic Forms as living potencies. Jung's thinking function operates here: logical analysis revealing underlying patterns. The third explicate dimension providing architecture to dynamic substrate.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as third explicate causal position providing structural principle that organizes material substrate energized by efficient power toward final completion. Completes (2-3) dyadic core.
- **Operational symbolics:** Blueprint (architectural plan); DNA double helix (genetic form); mathematical equation (structural law); geometric shape (pattern); musical score (formal structure)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as pattern-principle: Formal cause is what pattern/structure something instantiates (Apollo-form for statue, oak-form for acorn, mathematical equation for physical law). Provides intelligibility through structure - form makes matter comprehensible. Contains both universal (the Form itself) and particular (this instantiation). The "what structure" as answer to morphological analysis.
- **Consciousness structure:** Pattern-recognizing consciousness; awareness grasping formal structures; the "intelligibility" of phenomena
- **Key principles:** Morphe (Form): The pattern/structure organizing matter; Structural Principle: Architecture making intelligible; Jungian Thinking: Logical analysis and categorical organization; Pattern Recognition: Identifying underlying formal structures; Blueprint Function: Form as template guiding actualization; Intelligibility: Structure enabling comprehension; Universal-Particular: Form as both general type and specific instantiation
- **Architectural function:** Third explicate position in causal quaternary - the mediating structure that shapes raw material (1) energized by efficient cause (2) into determinate form. Formal cause provides intelligibility - without it, we have stuff in motion but no pattern. The synthetic moment where material and efficient unite in structured actuality. Mirrors Level 0's archetypal three - the SYNTHESIS, the mediating agent.
