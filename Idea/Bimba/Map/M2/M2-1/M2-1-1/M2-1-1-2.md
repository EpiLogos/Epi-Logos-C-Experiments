# M2-1-1-2 · Efficient cause (how)

> The motive force - value-driven energy that moves reality from potential to actual

## Detail
- **Description:** Efficient cause is action principle - where potential becomes actual. But Whitehead shows this isn't mechanical - each actual occasion is self-determining, creating its own efficient causation through prehension (affective grasping). Jung's feeling function operates here: valuation creating energy for movement. Not just "what makes it move" but "what makes it matter" - care and concern as motivating power. The second explicate dimension bringing dynamism to substrate.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as second explicate causal position providing dynamic power that actualizes material potential according to formal patterns toward final ends. First position in (2-3) dyadic core.
- **Operational symbolics:** Sculptor (productive agent); hammer (tool of making); heart (seat of feeling-energy); lightning (sudden activation); flowing river (continuous efficacy)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as active power: Efficient cause is what brings about change (sculptor for statue, parent for child, desire for action). Provides the push/pull making potency actual. Contains both agent (who acts) and energy (power to act). The "how" as answer to productive/generative analysis. In Kashmir Shaivism, Jnana Shakti is knowing-power - cognition itself as efficacious.
- **Consciousness structure:** Affectively-charged awareness; consciousness energized by care and value; the "mattering" of phenomena
- **Key principles:** Kinesis (Motion): The productive power bringing about change; Active Potency: Capacity to actualize and produce effects; Jungian Feeling: Affective valuation determining what matters; Jnana Shakti: Knowing-power where cognition and action unite; Productive Analysis: Answering "what produces this?"; Energetic Principle: The push/pull creating movement; Agency: The doing and making that actualizes potential
- **Architectural function:** Second explicate position in causal quaternary - the activating power putting material substrate into motion. Efficient cause is the "how" - the mechanism of change. Jnana Shakti reveals this as cognitive-affective power: knowing and acting are inseparable. The dynamic complement to material cause's static substrate. Mirrors Level 0's archetypal two - the METHOD, the dyadic engine.
- **Practical applications:** Causal Analysis: Identifying productive agents and mechanisms; Motivational Understanding: Recognizing what drives action; Energy Work: Directing psychic/physical energy effectively; Affective Intelligence: Using feeling as guide to value; Agency Recognition: Seeing who/what produces effects; Creative Process: Understanding generative mechanisms; Will Cultivation: Developing capacity for efficacious action
- **Resonances:** Aristotle: Efficient cause as to kinoun - that which produces motion; Jung: Feeling function - valuation, affective judgment; Kashmir Shaivism: Jnana Shakti as cognitive power; Whitehead: Efficient causation through prehension; Schopenhauer: Will as the underlying productive force; Bergson: Élan vital as creative evolutionary impulse; Sculptor Example: Sculptor is efficient cause of statue's coming-to-be
