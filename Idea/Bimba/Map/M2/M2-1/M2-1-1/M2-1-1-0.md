# M2-1-1-0 · Primordial cause

> The unmanifest causal field - pure potentiality before differentiation into specific causes

## Detail
- **Description:** This is Being (Sat) itself - not a being with a cause, but causation's source. The four causes don't yet differentiate but exist as pure potential. Absolute substrate from which material, efficient, formal, and final causes will emerge as four aspects of one reality. Following Kashmir Shaivism, Sat is the eternal IS-ness - not coming to be (which would require cause) but always already being. The causal plenitude that Aristotle's Physics requires to ground the explanatory chain. The [0↔1] oscillation here: uncaused being (0) manifesting as first cause (1).
- **Operational essence:** Operates as implicate causal ground - the substrate from which all causal operations emerge without itself being caused. Forms potential pole mirroring Level 0's archetypal zero.
- **Operational symbolics:** Unmoved mover (center that moves all); eternal flame (self-sustaining); ground of being (foundation requiring no foundation); alpha point (causal origin)
- **Internal structure:** Organized as pre-differentiated unity containing all four causal modes implicitly: The material substrate (what could be), efficient power (self-actuating capacity), formal principle (pure actuality), and final end (self-sufficient completion) exist here without distinction. This is the philosophical equivalent of zero in archetypal mathematics - the pregnant void from which causal quaternary emerges.
- **Consciousness structure:** Pre-reflective being-awareness; pure isness before differentiation into knower and known; primordial sensation
- **Key principles:** Uncaused Cause: Self-subsistent reality requiring no external explanation; Causal Plenitude: Contains all four causes undifferentiated; Being (Sat): Pure IS-ness before differentiation into beings; Aristotelian Ground: Unmoved mover preventing infinite regress; Self-Sufficient: Causa sui - cause of itself, complete in itself; Pre-Differentiated Unity: Four causes exist here implicitly, not explicitly; Primordial Sensation: Brute facticity of existence before explanation
- **Architectural function:** Implicate ground preventing infinite causal regress - Aristotle's recognition that explanation requires uncaused cause. Position 0 holds all four causes in undivided unity: simultaneously material (substrate of all), efficient (self-moving), formal (pure act), and final (self-sufficient). The quaternary exists here implicitly, not yet differentiated into four distinct causal types. Mirrors Level 0's archetypal zero as primordial potential.
- **Practical applications:** Explanatory Ground: Where questioning ends - the unquestionable ground; Meditation Object: Pure being-awareness before conceptualization; Philosophical Foundation: Starting point for causal analysis; Phenomenological Reduction: The "that it is" before "what it is"; Existential Recognition: Sheer existence as primary wonder; Causal Archaeology: Excavating back to uncaused source; Ontological Inquiry: Being as such, not this or that being
- **Resonances:** Aristotle: Unmoved mover as first cause, pure actuality (energeia); Kashmir Shaivism: Sat as eternal being, Shiva as pure existence; Aquinas: Ipsum esse subsistens - subsistent being itself; Heidegger: Sein (being) vs. Seiende (beings) - ontological difference; Jung: Sensation function grasping immediate givenness; Parmenides: "What is, is" - being simply is, cannot not-be; Spinoza: Substance as causa sui, self-causing nature
