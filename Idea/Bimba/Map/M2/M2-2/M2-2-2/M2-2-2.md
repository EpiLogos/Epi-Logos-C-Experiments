## Detail
- **Description:** The Impure Tattvas encompass the entire domain of matter and mind as experienced by the limited Purusha through prakriti and its evolutes.
- **Operational essence:** Domain of prakriti where consciousness appears as insentient matter and limited mental faculties
- **Internal structure:** Twenty-four tattvas organized in five subcategories: Prakriti root (#2-2-2-0), Antahkarana (#2-2-2-1), Jnanendriyas (#2-2-2-2), Karmendriyas (#2-2-2-3), Tanmatras (#2-2-2-4), Mahabhutas (#2-2-2-5)
- **Consciousness structure:** Consciousness veiled by matter, operating through psychophysical instruments
- **Key principles:** Prakriti as root matter; Three gunas dynamics; Evolution of psychophysical instruments; Gross and subtle elements; Complete embodiment structure
- **Architectural function:** Manifests the complete domain of mind-matter experience for the limited individual through Samkhya categories
- **Practical applications:** Understanding embodiment; Elemental meditation practices; Sense withdrawal techniques; Mental faculty purification; Gross to subtle yoga practices
- **Resonances:** Yetzirah and Assiah in Kabbalah; Nirmanakaya in Buddhism; Material cosmos in Gnosticism; Samkhya 24 principles; Mulk and Mithal in Sufism
