# M2-2 · 36 Tattvas System

> Comprehensive metaphysical framework mapping the progressive manifestation and reabsorption of Supreme Consciousness through 36 principles in dual aspect

## Detail
- **Description:** The 36 Tattvas system represents Kashmir Shaivism's comprehensive map of reality, describing how Supreme Consciousness (Paramasiva) manifests through 36 progressive stages of self-limitation to create the universe and individual experience. Each tattva exists in dual aspect - descending (pravritti) where consciousness contracts into form, and ascending (nivritti) where awareness expands back to its source. This creates 72 distinct states or perspectives, forming a complete spiritual cosmology and liberation methodology.
- **Operational essence:** Dual-directional consciousness mapping system revealing divine self-limitation and self-recognition processes
- **Internal structure:** Three primary divisions (mod3): Pure Tattvas/5 (#2-2-0), Pure-Impure Tattvas/7 (#2-2-1), and Impure Tattvas/24 (#2-2-2), each with descending (pravritti) and ascending (nivritti) aspects creating 72 total states
- **Consciousness structure:** Non-dualistic framework integrating all levels of reality from Paramasiva to Prithvi through systematic unfoldment and enfoldment
- **Key principles:** Progressive self-limitation of consciousness; Dual-aspect manifestation and realization; Non-dualistic integration of all reality levels; Divine play of concealment and revelation (maya-lila); Systematic consciousness cartography through modular unfoldment
- **Architectural function:** Provides complete cartography of consciousness from pure awareness to gross matter, with both descending (manifestation) and ascending (realization) pathways
- **Practical applications:** Tattva shuddhi meditation practices; Consciousness state navigation; Spiritual practice hierarchy (upaya); Understanding embodiment process; Liberation pathway mapping (moksha-marga)
- **Resonances:** Kabbalah Tree of Life and Four Worlds; Hermetic Planes of Existence; Buddhist Skandhas and Ayatanas; Samkhya 25 Tattvas; Gnostic Aeons and Archons
- **QL category:** Bridge
