# M2-2-1 · Shuddhashuddha Tattvas

> Intermediate reality levels where universal consciousness contracts into individual experience through maya and self-imposed limitations

## Detail
- **Description:** The Pure-Impure Tattvas constitute the limiting instruments through which infinite consciousness contracts itself to appear as finite individual souls through maya and the five kanchukas.
- **Operational essence:** Domain of cosmic limitation where the infinite becomes finite through systematic contraction
- **Internal structure:** Seven tattvas in mod8 structure: Maya (#2-2-1-0/1), Kala-limitation (#2-2-1-2), Vidya (#2-2-1-3), Raga (#2-2-1-4), Kala-time (#2-2-1-5), Niyati (#2-2-1-6), Purusha (#2-2-1-7). Maya as 0/1 represents the non-dual veiling principle linking pure to impure.
- **Consciousness structure:** Transitional consciousness experiencing progressive limitation from universal to individual
- **Key principles:** Maya as divine veiling power; Five kanchukas (sheaths) of limitation; Contraction of infinite powers; Formation of individual soul (anu); Bridge between universal and particular
- **Architectural function:** Creates the interface between absolute and empirical reality through progressive veiling via maya and the five kanchukas
- **Practical applications:** Understanding ego formation; Transcending limitations in meditation; Working with kanchukas in practice; Maya recognition exercises; Individual soul purification
- **Resonances:** Briah in Kabbalah; Sambhogakaya in Buddhism; Demiurge realm in Gnosticism; Hiranyagarbha in Vedanta; Barzakh in Sufism
- **QL category:** Bridge
- **QL position:** 1
- **QL operator types:** structural
- **Context frame:** Mod3 - trika
