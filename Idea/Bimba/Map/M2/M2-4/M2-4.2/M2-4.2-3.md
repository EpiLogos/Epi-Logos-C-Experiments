# M2-4.2-3 · Zuhd

> Progressive liberation from worldly attachments and desires, embodying prophetic simplicity

## Detail
- **Description:** Zuhd progressively liberates the heart from all forms of attachment, creating the emptiness necessary for divine presence. It embodies the prophetic model of simplicity.
- **Operational essence:** Freedom from both material and subtle forms of acquisitiveness
- **Internal structure:** Three types: Awam - neither joy at gain nor grief at loss; Khawas - renouncing praise/honor; Khawas al-Khawas - renouncing consciousness of renunciation
- **Key principles:** Prophetic simplicity; Liberation from acquisitiveness; Preparation for divine filling
- **Architectural function:** Purifying force removing obstacles to divine realization through systematic detachment
