## Detail

- **Operational essence:** Dynamic faith manifesting as consistent spiritual effort despite obstacles
- **Internal structure:** Three categories: Awam - patience with physical trials; Khawas - patience from evil desires; Khawas al-Khawas - patience in all states
- **Key principles:** Active submission not passive resignation; Alchemy of acceptance; Following archetype of Job
- **Architectural function:** Alchemical transformer converting trials into spiritual advancement
