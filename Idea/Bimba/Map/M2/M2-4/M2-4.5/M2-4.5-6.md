# M2-4.5-6 · Ishim

> Leadership and strategic powers for those who guide others. These names develop executive consciousness, strategic thinking, and the ability to manifest collective visions while maintaining spiritual alignment and serving the divine plan.

## Detail
- **Description:** The Seventh Divine Choir empowers leadership in service of the divine plan. From Mebahiah (eternal clarity) to Anauel (infinite goodness manifesting as prosperity), these names were invoked by Jewish mystics seeking to lead their communities through difficult times. The Zohar associates this choir with the sephirah of Netzach (Victory), emphasizing endurance in manifesting divine will. This choir teaches the delicate balance between spiritual authority and human humility, enabling leaders to serve as clear channels for divine guidance without becoming corrupted by personal power.
