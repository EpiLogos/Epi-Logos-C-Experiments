## Detail
- **Description:** The dynamic, ongoing temporal process through which meanings sediment - the gerund "constituting" rather than completed "constitution." Where living insight gradually hardens into habitual understanding, where the fresh becomes familiar, where the explicit becomes implicit. Husserl's Sedimentierung as active process, Heidegger's temporality as ecstatic structure. The documents: "sediment is not behind us chronologically but beneath us structurally" - the past as ground rather than bygone.
- **Operational essence:** Enacts the temporal thickness of consciousness - retention (just-past held in awareness), primal impression (now-point), protention (about-to-be anticipated). But operates at a deeper level: how the very structures of meaning-giving themselves sediment over time, becoming invisible background that shapes future meaning-constitution without being explicitly recognized. This is operative history (Wirkungsgeschichte) - tradition working through us even when we don't recognize it, sedimented wisdom and sedimented prejudice equally.
- **Architectural function:** Occupies the #2 position - the vibrational, dynamic layer where Parashakti's cosmic pulsation operates. If #1 Constitution is the inaugural cut, #2 Temporal Sedimentation is the rhythmic vibration that propagates from that cut, creating waves of meaning-sedimentation rippling through time. This is spanda as temporal process - the heartbeat of meaning-making, the ongoing pulsation between novelty and habit, insight and sedimentation.
