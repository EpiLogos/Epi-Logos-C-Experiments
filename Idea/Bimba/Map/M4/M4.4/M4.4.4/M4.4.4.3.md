## Detail
- **Description:** The sedimented structures themselves - cultural formations, symbolic systems, traditional wisdom, embodied habits, language as inherited. What temporal sedimentation has produced: relatively stable patterns of meaning that precede individuals and outlast them. The "second nature" that feels like first nature. Institution in Merleau-Ponty's sense - not just social institutions but the institutED. Language, culture, body-habits, conceptual frameworks as received yet requiring ongoing reactivation.
- **Operational essence:** Operates as the received symbolic order - the langue before parole, the cultural archive before individual utterance, the traditional wisdom before personal insight. This is the "already-there" (Vorhanden) that every individual is thrown into: you don't choose your native language, your cultural symbols, your bodily hexis, your sedimented habits. They choose you - or more accurately, you emerge already-constituted by them. Yet Institution isn't deterministic: it provides possibilities (and constraints) for individual appropriation.
- **Architectural function:** Occupies the #3 position - the symbolic, formal, represented layer where Mahamaya operates. If #1 Constitution is inaugural act and #2 Sedimentation is temporal process, #3 Symbolic Body is crystallized product - meaning made visible, public, transmissible. This is where private intentions become public expressions, where individual insights enter collective discourse, where the invisible becomes visible through symbolic forms. The pivot point: institution both concludes (sedimentation complete) and initiates (providing ground for new appropriation).
