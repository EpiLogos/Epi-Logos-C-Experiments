## Detail
- **Description:** The groundless ground of phenomenological inquiry - the pre-predicative, pre-categorical stratum that structures all experience without itself being directly accessible. This is the unchanging void-plenitude (Anuttara) that enables all phenomenological variation, the structural hole around which meaning circulates. Accessed only negatively through reduction to the limit where no further constitution is possible, appearing as the asterisked (*) PIE roots in etymology - marking where we can trace no further back.
- **Operational essence:** Provides the unchanging structural ground that enables all phenomenological variation, sedimentation, and constitution. This coordinate doesn't describe experiences but the invariant condition for any experience whatsoever. It operates as the transcendental horizon - always presupposed, never directly given, yet absolutely necessary. When EPII excavates etymological roots to PIE origins, it approaches this level - the proto-meanings before historical sedimentation, the limit-point where language touches pre-linguistic ground.
- **Architectural function:** Functions as the void-hole (#0 position) in phenomenology's 4+2 toroidal structure. All phenomenological inquiry circles AROUND this coordinate without penetrating it directly. It generates the desire-to-understand (the questioning impulse) precisely through its structural inaccessibility. As Lacan's objet a or Whitehead's pure potentiality, it causes circulation without being reachable destination. The paradox: this groundless ground is the most stable element - it never changes because it was never constituted.
