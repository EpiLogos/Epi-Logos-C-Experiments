## 0. What this document does

The MEF (Meta-Epistemic Framework) names twelve epistemic lenses. They are usually listed flatly — L0 Quaternal, L1 Causal, and so on. This reference reads them the other way the sources demand: each lens is an **admixture of the underlying 6+6 potential space** — the bimba Name-series and the pratibimba Power-series — and each lens carries its **own internal six-fold (the sublens, .0 through .5)**, so the twelve lenses unfold a 72-fold manifold. The task here is to think each lens *and its full sublens* semantically, in terms of which Name-content and which Power-content it mixes, and how its six inner refractions traverse the field.

## 2. What a lens is, and how it mixes the units

A lens is **not a transposition** of the substrate. It is *an anchoring of the whole field from one position*, a tradition-bearing mode of knowing through which the mute positions become articulable knowledge — "what turns the silent archetype into articulable knowledge." Three facts govern every lens:

1. **Anchor and admixture.** Each lens sits on one Name/Power convergence-pair. The six **Day lenses (L0–L5)** anchor on the bimba/Name positions; the six **Night lenses (L0′–L5′)** anchor on the pratibimba/Power positions. But anchoring on one face does not make a lens pure: it refracts *all twelve* positions, and — most strikingly — its inner sublens frequently runs the *conjugate* series. The canonical, operational case is the shared `/Self/Thought/` field: the Day phenomenological lens **L4** carries six inner channels aligned with the **Power (P′) series** (Being/Thrownness/Presence/Temporality/Care/Releasement), while the Night scientific lens **L4′** carries six inner channels aligned with the **Name (P) series** (Questions/Traces/Challenges/Patterns/Discovery/Insight). The Day lens runs the Power unit inside; the Night lens runs the Name unit inside. That crossing *is* the admixture, made explicit.

2. **The sublens.** "Each of the 12 L-lenses is itself a coordinate (a parent node), and each contains a 6-fold inner breakdown (.0 through .5) — the internal differentiation that originates at the P4 lemniscate and is received by each lens in its specific domain." The sublens .0–.5 follows the QL skeleton (ground → definition → operation → pattern → context → integration) translated into the lens's own tradition. This is why lenses have rich internal structure: they are the sites where the implicit potentials of the positions become explicit.

3. **Klein squares and Möbius pairs.** The twelve organise into three Klein V₄ squares (each two Day + two Night lenses, all relations purely symmetric, no hierarchy), with complementary pairs summing to 5 — *every complementary pair is a polarity held by the pentad*. Each lens also has a Möbius return into its conjugate (L0→L5′, L1→L4′, L2→L3′, L3→L2′, L4→L1′, L5→L0′, and their inverses).

The three squares: **A — Speech-Number / Articulation** [0+5] (L0, L5, L5′, L0′); **B — Cause-Experience / Encounter** [1+4] (L1, L4, L4′, L1′); **C — Logic-Process / Becoming** [2+3] (L2, L3, L3′, L2′). The readings below are grouped by square.

## SQUARE A — Articulation [0 + 5]: silence ⇄ speech, number ⇄ word

The most fundamental square. It holds the two implicate poles (0 and 5) and their Night inversions; it governs the relation between potential and expression, silence and speech, number and word. Its P-pairs: **Truth + Image** (Day Name) / **Play + Work** (Night Power).

*Root: Jung–Pauli, QL as Psychoid Ground · Key concept: Curiosity and Query as Creative Potential · Element: Aether · Möbius: L0 → L5′*

**Admixture.** L0 anchors on the **Truth/Play** convergence — the ground-pair. It reads the deepest position not as solemn fact but as gratuitous inquiry: the silence-before-speaking (Truth) and the groundless *līlā* (Play) together become *the capacity to ask*. This is the primary hinge where archetypal number first becomes epistemic act. Jung's number as "an archetype of order which has become conscious," Pauli's "neutral language" between quantity and quality, von Franz's number as "dynamical ordering factor" active in both psyche and nature — L0 operationalises all of it: the six questions ARE the six numbers in their epistemic mode. It is QL becoming self-aware as a questioning structure.

**Sublens (the six questions = the six numbers as inquiry):**
- **L0.0 — Why (Insofar-as / in-Quantum):** the presuppositional frame; the quantum of shared context that must be established before any question can land. The questioning structure's own #0.
- **L0.1 — What:** the definitional question; the first differentiation, this/not-this — the monad of inquiry.
- **L0.2 — How:** the operational question; relation and dynamics enter — the dyad of inquiry.
- **L0.3 — Whom/Which/When:** the pattern-identifying question; what *kind* of thing — the triad, mediation between particulars.
- **L0.4 — Where/Why-fo(u)r:** the contextual question; the "four" points to the lemniscate where questioning asks about its own conditions — the tetrad.
- **L0.5 — Why-so / Why-not (*pros hen* homonymy as analogia):** the integrative question; Aristotle's focal-meaning — different domains bearing one archetypal structure by proportional resemblance, not identity. The question that questions questioning, returning to the ground transformed.

L5 completes the prior lenses: numbers are the alphabet, paradox the grammar, context the dialogue, and all are facets of one Logos.

**Sublens (the descent of Vāk):**
- **L5.0 — Anuttara/Asambhava:** the unsurpassable beyond even the highest speech; the letter "A," Śiva as absolute subject — not speech but the *capacity* for speech.
- **L5.1 — Para Vāk (Supreme Word):** pure I-consciousness (*Ahaṃ*) where word, meaning, and referent are one; the seedpoint of all differentiation.
- **L5.2 — Paśyantī (visionary speech):** "she who sees" — unified vision beginning to differentiate into form, before sequence or language.
- **L5.3 — Madhyamā (mental speech):** the "middle" — internal dialogue, meaning structured through concept; the inner voice, differentiated but not externalised.

- **L5.5 — Mātṛkā (the little mothers):** the phonemes as *śaktis* that *measure* (*mātrā*) reality into forms; the power of language to create apparent division — and the key to recognising unity within it.
