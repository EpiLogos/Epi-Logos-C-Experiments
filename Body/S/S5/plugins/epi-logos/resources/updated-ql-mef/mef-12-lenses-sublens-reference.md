## 2. What a lens is — tonic, rotation, sublens

A lens is **a tonic that begins a chromatic scale**, and a chromatic scale anchored at any note splits into **two whole-tone scales of six** — the Name hexad and the Power hexad. So **every lens hosts the entire 6+6**, rotated to begin at its anchor. The lens's tonic sits at the head of one hexad: the **Name-face** for the six Day lenses (tonic a bimba note), the **Power-face** for the six Night lenses (tonic a pratibimba note). This is the refraction: all twelve archetypes are present in every lens, but *which Name and which Power ground the field* — and the whole cascade that follows — is unique to each.

**The sublens is this rotation made internal.** Each lens has six inner positions (.0–.5) that follow the QL skeleton — ground, definition, operation, pattern, context, integration — and because the lens grounds *at its anchor*, sublens **.k sits at absolute position (anchor + k)**, carrying that position's Name-form and Power-form. The lens's tradition-content at each node (e.g. L0's *Why/What/How…*, L2's *IS/IS-NOT/BOTH/NEITHER/SILENCE*) is its specific articulation of that rotated Name/Power pair. The §1–2 sublens descriptions and the Name/Power note-tabulation are therefore *the same six positions* — given together below.

**Three concomitant grains** (most primordial first):

1. **The 6+6 (chromatic / two whole-tone scales)** — the full constellation, both faces at all six rotated positions.
2. **The 8-fold (Barry Harris)** — prior to the diatonic, more "whole": the **inner-fours** of each hexad (.1–.4) sound (4 Names + 4 Powers); the **outer-twos** (.0, .5 of each hexad) are held as the **four anchor-nodes** — empty cymatic-organising stillnesses, not sounded content.
3. **The 7-fold (diatonic)** — cut from the 8-fold by the **seven context-frames**: 3 Names → conjugate-cross → 4 Powers, with the eighth note withdrawn as complexification-hook.

**Day/Night twins.** The Day lens Lₖ and the Night lens Lₖ′ share an index, so they share the **same rotated 6+6, 8-fold, and diatonic** — differing only in which face grounds (Day = Name-led tonic, Night = Power-led tonic). Six rotations × two faces = twelve lenses. (This is distinct from the Klein-square Möbius pairing, where each lens pairs with the one summing to 5 — L0↔L5′, L1↔L4′, L2↔L3′; both relations hold at once.)

The twelve organise into three Klein V₄ squares — **A Articulation** [0+5], **B Encounter** [1+4], **C Becoming** [2+3] — presented below with same-index twins adjacent.
