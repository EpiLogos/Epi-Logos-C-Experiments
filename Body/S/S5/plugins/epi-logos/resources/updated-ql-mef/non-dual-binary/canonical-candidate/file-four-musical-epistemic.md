## §0 — The Foundational Ratios

The musical derivation begins from the standing identity:

$$\frac{0}{1} + \frac{1}{0} = \frac{1}{1} \equiv 100\%$$

The expression must be read as the matheme reads it, not as bare arithmetic. 0/1 and 1/0 are the two directional traversals of the relation that File 1's closing fraction-reading earned: the void in ratio to unity, collapsing toward zero; unity in ratio to the void, opening toward divergence. $$100\% = 2^6 + 6^2 = 64 + 36$$

and the proportion between the two components reduces:

$$\frac{64}{36} = \frac{16}{9} = \left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{2^4}{3^2}$$

This single ratio admits several coherent readings, and the multiplicity is itself structural — the matheme's holographic character showing as a convergence of angles on one number-relation. Read as $(4/3)^2$, it is the doubled fourth: the manifestation-span applied twice, once per conjugate pass of the double cover. ## §1 — The Two Chromatic Substrates

The matheme admits two parallel chromatic derivations, each using one of its own foundational ratios as generator. Neither is prior. The **chromatic basis** steps by the epogdoon, 9/8 — the tick-quantum become stepping-quantum; in twelve-tone equal temperament, two semitones per step. The **fifths basis** steps by the perfect fifth, 3/2 — the harmonic-architecture generator; seven semitones per step. Both produce the complete twelve-note palette. They differ in traversal-ordering, not in substrate-content: the chromatic basis surfaces adjacency, advance, and the cross-helix pulse at its minimum; the fifths basis surfaces tonal architecture and the same pulse at its maximum. Two readings of one substrate — which is precisely what bimba and pratibimba taught us to expect of any matheme-projection.

## §2 — The Universal Pairing Grammar

**Family A — Being — `ADJACENTLY_ARTICULATES`:**

$$A = \{(0,1),\ (2,3),\ (4,5)\}$$

The natural dyads: each position paired with its immediate companion, ground with first articulation, operation with pattern, context with synthesis. **Family B — Becoming — `MIRRORS_COMPLEMENT`:**

$$B = \{(0,5),\ (1,4),\ (2,3)\}$$

The mirror-pairs across the hexad's centre of symmetry, each summing to 5: ground mirrored in synthesis, definition mirrored in context, operation mirrored in pattern. In the chromatic basis the B-pairs sound as minor seventh (C–A♯, the 16/9 totality-span itself), tritone (D–G♯), and whole-tone (E–F♯); in the fifths basis as major seventh (C–B), minor third (G–E, as pitch-class interval), and perfect fifth (D–A) — three of tonal harmony's four primary intervals surfacing directly from the mirror-grammar.

**Family C — Knowing-Unknowing — `CROSSES_KNOWING_LIMIT`:**

$$C = \{(1,2),\ (3,4),\ (5,0)\}$$

The crossing-pairs: each steps from one natural dyad into the next, across the boundary that the A-grammar establishes. Definition crosses into operation, pattern crosses into context, and — the pair that names the family — synthesis crosses into ground: the (5,0) pair is the §5→§0′ enrichment itself, the achieved whole crossing the limit of its own knowing back into the ground it cannot know as content. In the chromatic basis the C-pairs sound as whole-tone steps, with the cycle-close A♯→C′ crossing the register boundary — the enrichment audible as the step that lands in the next octave. In the fifths basis they sound as perfect fourths, with the cycle-close B→C′ as the minor second — the leading-tone's semitone pull, the knowing-limit heard as the smallest and most directed of all melodic tensions.

The pair (2,3) appears in both A and B, and the double membership is canonical, not accidental. Positions 2 and 3 — Word and Logos, operation and pattern, the self-inverting core that every prior file located at the field's middle — are adjacent Being and mirror Becoming at once: the midpoint of the hexad, where the mirror-symmetry folds on itself and adjacency coincides with complementarity.
