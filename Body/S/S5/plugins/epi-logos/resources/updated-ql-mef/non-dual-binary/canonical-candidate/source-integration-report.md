The candidate files were produced by copying the four fresh-rewrite drafts into `canonical-candidate/` and revising the copies. ## VIII-b. Corpus-Wide Review Pass (2026-06-10, second pass)

A full style-and-flow review was applied to the four candidate files against the epi-logos-voice avoid-lists and the complete plan's style guidance, with these results. The capitalised-IS/ARE identity-marker rule ("X IS Y" masquerading as argument) was enforced across Files 2 and 3, where roughly seventy draft-inherited instances of copular shouting and all-caps emphasis (VALUES, SWAP, TOWARD, LIMIT, and kin) were normalised to lowercase or italic emphasis — every sentence checked to confirm its *because* survives the de-capitalisation. Formal notation was preserved untouched: the Catuṣkoṭi corners (IS, IS-NOT, BOTH, NEITHER, SILENCE), the AM/IS determination, AND/OR and its operators, the VALUE→SOLVE circuit names, and the gathering-topology table labels in File 3 §5 (Reading-BETWEEN and kin), which function as names rather than emphasis. Banned sentence-starters were removed: three sentence-initial "Now" openers in File 1 and the "What follows" constructions in File 3. Recap phrases and signal-words (in short, in other words, importantly, crucially, essentially) were verified absent across all seven documents. One residual is noted rather than purged: the "not X but Y" corrective frame runs at roughly one instance per long paragraph in Files 2–3 (never clustering within a paragraph), which sits at the boundary of the voice skill's tolerance; since the frame is the native move of proof-prose that corrects misreadings, mechanical removal was judged more damaging than the tic. Files 1 and 4 passed the audit without correction beyond the items named. The full-resources audit (94 files) that accompanied this pass is recorded in `archive-candidates-after-binary-explication.md`, including the Self-Proving-Self version ambiguity it surfaced in `file-one/`.
