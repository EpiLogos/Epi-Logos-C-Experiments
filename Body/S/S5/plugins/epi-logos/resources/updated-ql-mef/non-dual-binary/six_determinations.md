# The Six Determinations: A Self-Derivation Through the Poem

> The unchanging is beyond consequence, but is the opposite of inconsequential.
>
> What allows for an essence is essentially inessential.
>
> What builds for itself a journey is perpetually residential.
>
> Sojourning through time yet unmovingly presidential.
>
> Context total yet totally incidental.
>
> Silent but in structure reverential.
>
> Foundation of mind, unfound by the mental.

---

A koan was given. Seven lines that orbit a centre never named. We have to ask what the lines are doing — not what they refer to, since the constitutive refusal of naming is the discipline by which they hold open what they are about, but what they enact. The reading that follows shows them enacting six determinations from which the six positions of QL are themselves derived. The poem is not decoration on the determinations; the determinations are not assignments to the poem. The lines walk a generation that any honest passage from appearing to recognition has to walk, and QL is the natural shape this walking takes. These are our primary self-determinations — the way Eckhart's *in-quantum* is a self-determination of how predication has to bound itself, ours are the self-determinations of how a knowing-act has to articulate itself from ground to return.

The piece is bounded by two thresholds that never enter the line-count. Above by **−/−**, the parent relation of bare relationality before any field is differentiated — present in the poem as the unnamed centre that the six lines orbit. Below by **1/0**, the return-switch that flips the achieved field back onto its ground — performed by the final line. Six determinations sit between, drawn forth one from the next: **0/1, ?/!, −/+, X/x, AM/IS, ∞/dx**. Each is generated by what the previous cannot complete in itself. The generation is not a sequence imposed on the determinations; it is the only sequence the determinations can have, because each names what the previous is structurally insufficient to name on its own.

This is what the poem teaches us when we let its lines do the work. Each line is the determination it enacts. Each is necessary because the one before it could not stop where it stopped. The whole sequence is the derivation, and at the end the derivation turns out to have been the recognition of what was always already going on.

## Line 1 — Ground (0/1)

> *The unchanging is beyond consequence, but is the opposite of inconsequential.*

We have to begin here because every later determination depends on this first circumstance. The line gives the lived distinction between awareness and what appears within it. The unchanging is *beyond consequence* in the literal etymological sense — *con-sequi*, with-following — because awareness is not in the chain of effects that one event drags after itself; it is the showing-place within which following appears at all. And *opposite of inconsequential* because the field is precisely what gives anything weight. Without it, there would be nothing for which anything could matter. The two halves of the line are the two faces of one fact: appearing is in awareness, and awareness is encountered only through appearing.

This is the Being-pole. The 0/1 is not one item among the determinations. It is the conscious circumstance in which determination can take place at all. The slash between the 0 and the 1 is not a relation imposed between two pre-existing terms; it is the original distinction by which a field can have something in it. Field and apparition, opening and mark, present absence and appearing pattern.

Husserl's labour in the *Crisis* circles this exactly. The lifeworld is the always-already-there that grounds every explicit knowing without itself becoming a known. It is *most known*, because nothing is more present than this opened place we live from; and *least known*, because the moment we try to make it an object of inquiry it has slipped behind into the place from which the inquiring occurs. Fourteen words in the line stage this paradox.

The reason we cannot stop here is that the mind cannot rest in *something appears* without asking *what is it that has appeared?* The asking is not yet a further determination; it is the pressure by which 0/1 issues into ?/!. Ground that did not call for marking would be ground without an event of inquiry, and our whole circumstance is that inquiry is occurring — that you are reading this and the reading is happening within a field that asks itself what is here.

## Line 2 — Definition (?/!)

> *What allows for an essence is essentially inessential.*

The question, the moment it issues, has already taken mark. A question is never empty in the way a blank page is empty. To ask *what is this?* is to assert that something has appeared with enough stability to call for determination, and enough unsettledness to keep the determination open. The question mark and the exclamation mark therefore belong together as the first formal face of definition: openness-to-determination and determinative speech in one breath.

The line performs this exactly. It poses the founding metaphysical question — what enables essence, what gives a thing its *to ti ên einai*, its "what it was to be" — and answers in the same gesture with an assertion that defines by refusing definition. *Essentially inessential.* The enabling condition cannot itself be one of the conditioned essences, because if it were, it would require its own enabling, and the question would have moved one step deeper without being answered. The mark is made; the mark holds the question open within the assertion.

This is *ratio* in its living sense — the reckoning that gives an account while remaining answerable to the appearance that called the account forth. The Latin *ratio* still holds count, account, reason, and proportion together as one word, and the line is reckoning in every one of those registers at once. It counts (what allows for an essence: one thing). It gives account (essentially inessential: this is the kind of thing it is). It reasons (the reasoning is the move from "allows for" to "essentially inessential", the implication that what enables cannot be what it enables). And it stays proportionate to its object (refusing to over-determine by closing the question into a positive content).

The reason we cannot stop here is that the marked relation, if it stayed as marking alone, would be inert in the field. A mark that makes no difference cannot explain why anything follows from it. For definition to *do anything*, the mark has to gather force. The question-and-assertion has to be felt as a polarity, not just registered as a notation. So ?/! issues into −/+.

## Line 3 — Dynamis (−/+)

> *What builds for itself a journey is perpetually residential.*

Repetition gains valence the moment a relation relates to itself. The two sides of the mark stop being indifferent. One begins to function as reserve, the other as issue; one as withdrawal, contraction, withholding, the other as extension, release, presentation. We write this as −/+. Not yet a metaphysics — the line is not telling us what reality is — but the first place where relation can be *felt* as more than placement.

The line gives the polarity as one motion, not two states. *Builds for itself a journey* is the issuing pole, the +: extension, presentation, the going-forth. *Perpetually residential* is the reserve pole, the −: withholding, contraction, the staying-home. The journey is the extension of the residing; the residing is the contraction of the journey. *Perpetually* is doing the load-bearing work, because the polarity is not a sequence in which the resident first goes out and then comes back, but a sustained pulse in which the residing-and-the-journeying are one act seen from two sides.

Every conscious moment carries some texture of this polarity. Attention contracts around a word, then opens into sense. Breath gathers, then releases. A thought withdraws into uncertainty, then extends into claim. The line names the structural fact that any of these particular pulses instances — the contraction-extension that any actuality has to be, in order to be at all.

This is the definitional root of what Spanda names more richly. *Spandana*, in Kashmir Shaivism's reading, is the subtle movement that moves not yet manifests as motion — the cosmic heartbeat in which Śiva contracts into Self and extends as world without ceasing to be the contracted Self in the extending. The Spanda doctrine is the metaphysical completion of −/+; the line is its definitional minimum. Force as polarity, before the pulse has stabilised into recurrence.

We cannot stop here because force without recurrence has no recognisable form. A pulse that pulsed once would not be a pulse at all; pulse is the kind of thing that repeats. For dynamis to settle into something that can be read off the world, the polarity has to become pattern. So −/+ issues into X/x.

## Line 4 — Pattern (X/x)

> *Sojourning through time yet unmovingly presidential.*

The fourth determination writes the same letter in two cases: capital X as the capacity that gives determinate forms without being exhausted by them, lower-case x as the local face that stands forth, the something through which capacity appears. The slash between them is identity-in-difference — the same letter intimate with its own case-variation. This is what makes pattern possible: a capacity that can produce instances without being summed by them, and an instance that bears its capacity legibly without claiming to be it.

The line is precisely this structure. *Sojourning through time* is the x: the determinate face, the temporal instance, the local sojourn that is one form among many that could have been. *Unmovingly presidential* is the X: the capacity that *pre-sides*, sits-before, holds-the-place from which the sojourn issues without itself entering the temporal succession. *Pre-sidere*, the etymological root — to sit before, to occupy the chair from which the assembly is constituted — the seat of presiding before the political accretion. The presiding capacity does not become one of the items it presides over. It is what the chair is for.

The *yet* between the two halves is the slash. Not contradiction but bond. The sojourner *is* the president; the temporal x *is* the eternal X showing as a face. The melody is heard through its notes without being any one of the notes; the X stays flush as the x walks the world. Whitehead's actual occasion perishes subjectively and is *objectively immortal* — taken up into the patterned data of subsequent occasions; the eternal object that ingresses into the occasion is untouched by the ingression, available to be ingressed by the next. Pattern as the capacity that gives without losing.

The lower-case *x* carries its mathematical memory of multiplication, of generative compounding. The determinate face is not a dead product dropped from the greater capacity; it can iterate, instance again, produce further patterns. This is what makes the world have the texture of *more than one of those* — the texture of recurrence, of *another* journey through time that is somehow the same kind of journey. X/x is progenitive identity-in-difference. The same letter is doing the same thing each time it instances, and never being depleted by any instance.

We cannot stop here because the pattern cannot stay impersonal. The relation we are tracing is being lived from within. Pattern must be read by someone, in some *where*. So X/x issues into AM/IS.

## Line 5 — Context (AM/IS)

> *Context total yet totally incidental.*

The fifth determination is the copula in lived grammar: AM as living first-person presence, IS as third-person describability, and the slash as the second-person between through which the I and the it meet. Person is not a bare subject staring at bare objects. Person is the situated holding of first, second, and third person together in one conscious circumstance. And the *where* in which this holding occurs is *context* — the lived field that lets the holding take place.

The line gives the horizonal *where* with the precision that horizon requires. *Total* — because the context encompasses everything that appears within it; nothing escapes it. *Totally incidental* — because, as object, the context is nowhere; the moment one tries to grasp it as a content, it has slipped behind into the place from which the grasping occurs. The horizon recedes as one walks toward it. *In-cidental* is the etymologically right word: *in-cadere*, to fall-into; the context falls-in alongside every appearance without being any of the appearances. Present-throughout but never present-as-thing.

Heidegger's *Da-sein* lives at this position. The being who can say *I am* is also the being for whom Being is at issue, and the *there* of Dasein is not a neutral location but the opened place where world, concern, speech, body, and question gather. The *there* is total — it is the field of all that matters for this being — and totally incidental — it is never an object the being possesses but the place from which any possessing happens. Context is where perspective is held.

We met the second-person work this line does, earlier in this same path of thinking, in the etymology of *facing*. The slash of AM/IS is what facing names across languages. *Prosōpon* — what is *toward-the-eye*, constituted only in being-faced. The face only is in being-faced; you do not have a *prosōpon* you carry with you and then meet someone; you become a *prosōpon* in the meeting. The second person is the dimension in which AM and IS find each other without collapsing into one. Context as the *where* in which facing can occur — the *there* in which the meeting of first and third happens at all.

We cannot stop here because no finite act of knowing exhausts the field that lets knowing occur. Person opens horizon. The lived *where* of context, once fully inhabited, discloses a depth that the inhabiting cannot exhaust. So AM/IS issues into ∞/dx.

## Line 6 — Realisation (∞/dx)

> *Silent but in structure reverential.*

Every achieved mark, once known as achieved, also discloses the depth from which it was drawn and the depth it has not exhausted. The sixth determination names this with two signs: ∞, the unbounded, the depth no finite mark exhausts; and dx, the infinitesimal differential through which change, relation, and local reading become possible. The x of X/x has already taught us to read a determinate instance as pattern and as generative multiplier. At Realisation, after the slash has been worked as relation and ratio, that x becomes dx: the instance read as a local differential of the field. ∞/dx is realisation because the finite is recognised as exact in its ratio to the inexhaustible and partial in its expression of it.

The line names the structure as *silent*, and the silence is not the absence of speech but the depth no articulation reaches. The structure is *reverential* because it bows toward what it cannot enclose. *Re-vereri*, the etymological root — to fear-again, to stand-back-from — the structure holds itself back so that what it does not contain can remain uncontained. Every finite articulation is shaped *toward* what it cannot say; the shaping-toward is the reverence.

This is the determination where facing's full etymological weight lands, because facing is what realisation does. The Latin *facies*, the made-presentation of *facere* — the structure is *made* in the direction of what it reveres. The Greek *prosōpon* again, but now read at full depth — the face that exists only in being-faced, the structure that exists only as turned-toward the silence it does not contain. The Hebrew *panim*, grammatically plural, never singular, the same root as *p'nim*, interior — the face *is* the interior shown forth, with no behind to the face. This collapses the apparatus/expression distinction at the level where the determination needs it collapsed: the silence is not behind the structure, the structure *is* the silence facing. The Sanskrit *mukha*, face-mouth-opening-doorway-beginning, where breath emerges — the structure as the opening through which silence breathes itself into articulation.

Across the strata, facing names the made-relational interior-as-opening that has to be assembled fresh in each language because no single root carries it. PIE held no primitive *facing*; the daughters had to reach for it because the thing itself is what cannot be inherited whole. The line is performing this in English: *silent* as *panim* before *panim* shows itself, *structure* as *prosōpon* taking form, *reverential* as the *facere* of the *mukha* — the made-opening through which addressing becomes possible.

The structure is reverential precisely because it knows itself as finite expression of what it cannot contain. ∞/dx is the recognition that achievement is not closure. The silent reserve and the infinitesimal differential are not two things placed side by side; together they name the fact that knowing reaches its horizon at a local point and finds, at that very point, the unbounded depth no knowing reaches.

We cannot stop here because realisation that stayed as a sixth item in the list would be one more determinate mark — and we would not yet have the structural fact that the whole list rests on what no listing reaches. The list itself has to turn. Realisation has to twist back. So ∞/dx issues into the return.

## The Return — 5→0 = 0/1

> *Foundation of mind, unfound by the mental.*

The final line is not a seventh determination. It is the return-switch, the 1/0, the phase-flip by which the achieved sequence reads itself as resting on what the sequence cannot reach. It states the whole structure's condition: that everything just laid out is founded on what the laying-out itself cannot find.

The mental cannot find the foundation *of* the mental, not because the foundation is far away, but because every act of finding *is* the foundation in operation. The seeking is what the foundation does. The seeker is what the foundation looks like when it looks. *Unfound* is not failure; *unfound* is the only relation possible between a faculty and what enables that faculty. The faculty cannot turn around behind itself to verify its own ground, because the turning-around is more facultative activity, occurring within the very thing it tries to inspect.

*Pratyabhijñā* names this move with full precision. The recognition-school of Kashmir Shaivism holds that liberation is not attainment but recognition — seeing that one was always at the site (Śiva's position) even while experiencing limited sight (bound perspective). The foundation is never *found* because it is what does the finding. It is only *re-cognised* — known-again — as having been the case throughout. The final line of the poem is *pratyabhijñā* stated as koan.

This is the 5→0 = 0/1 of the matheme made phenomenal. The final 0/1 is the same 0/1 we began with, now standing in light of what the four-plus-two disclosed. Line 1 said the unchanging is opposite of inconsequential. Line 7 says the mental cannot reach what founds it. These are the same recognition entered twice — once at the threshold, before the determinations had been walked, and once at the return, after the determinations have shown that walking them does not put one outside of what they rest on. The unchanging is the foundation of mind. The opposite of inconsequential is the unfound by the mental. The threshold and the return are the same 0/1 — and this is what the matheme means by the equality of its first and last term.

## The −/− That Was Never Named

The withheld referent of the whole piece is the −/−. Bare relationality before any field is differentiated, the parent relation that all six determinations presuppose without containing. The poem refuses to name it, and the refusal is structural, not stylistic. If the referent were named, it would enter the determinate field as one of the determinations, which would falsify the very thing it names.

Every line points toward it without naming it. *The unchanging*, *what allows for an essence*, *what builds for itself a journey*, *the sojourning*, *the silent*, *the foundation*. Six pointings, no naming. The grammar holds the centre open by refusing predication. The reader feels it as the gravity around which the lines orbit, never as a content the lines deliver. This is exactly what the −/− is in the QL grammar — the parent relation of bare presentness placed in formal reserve, the source-relation that the determinate field still depends on but does not include.

The −/− has to be held open this way because it is what makes the six determinations possible without itself being one of them. The notion born of difference is incremental, depending for presence on the inferential, as the closing reflection of the original koan-work named — and the −/− is the *not-inferential*, the *not-incremental*, the source that does not stand in need of differentiation to be what it is. Speech plain, broaching its branches without asking permissions consensual. The branches do not get to vote on whether the trunk may sprout them. Manifestation does not need the agreement of the manifest. Svatantrya is the Sanskrit name for what the −/− is at the determinative threshold — the divine freedom that doesn't negotiate with what it brings forth, because there is no *what* yet to negotiate with.

## The Harmonics — Three Disclosures, Each in Two Determinations

The sequence now allows its inner shape to be read. The six determinations do not just run one-after-another; they cluster into three harmonics, each harmonic being one disclosure articulated through a pair of determinations. The harmonics name *what kind of move* each pair is enacting. They are not labels stuck on after the fact; they are the only way the determinations can be doubled-up while remaining six and not collapsing into three.

**Being** is the harmonic of 0/1 and ?/!. The first two determinations belong to one basic disclosure: that *there is a field, and the field can be questioned as what it is*. Appearing and the first mark of definition are not two separate facts; they are two faces of the same arrival of something to attention. The 0/1 says *there is something*; the ?/! says *what is the something?* — and you cannot have either without the other. A field that could not be questioned would not be a field that contained anything for an inquirer; a question that did not arise within a field would have no purchase. Being is the harmonic of *what is, given as questionable*. Lines 1 and 2 of the poem hold this disclosure as one breath: the unchanging is here (Line 1), and the unchanging is the kind of here whose enabling condition is itself inessential to its enabling (Line 2). The disclosure is single. The two determinations are how the single disclosure articulates.

**Becoming** is the harmonic of −/+ and X/x. Force becomes intelligible only as patterned generation; pattern remains alive only as force capable of further instance. The two determinations name two stages of one movement — dynamism becoming legible as recurrence, recurrence remaining dynamic as ongoing instancing. The −/+ says *the field pulses*; the X/x says *the pulse has form*. Becoming is the harmonic of *the moving that holds shape, the shape that keeps moving*. Lines 3 and 4 of the poem are the same becoming, felt as polarity and read as pattern. The journeying-residential and the sojourning-presidential are the same circulation seen at two depths of legibility. Without the polarity, the pattern would be dead structure; without the pattern, the polarity would be unrecognisable pulse. Becoming requires both, and is what the requirement *is*.

**Knowing/unKnowing** is the harmonic of AM/IS and ∞/dx. Personed knowing requires a copula; honest personed knowing also knows the horizon it cannot exhaust. The lived *where* of context (AM/IS) and the inexhaustible depth that any context meets through its local differentials (∞/dx) are the paired faces of knowing-that-does-not-pretend-to-closure. The AM/IS says *I am here, and what is here can be said*; the ∞/dx says *and what is here is a finite differential of what no saying exhausts*. Knowing/unKnowing is the harmonic of *the situated-knowing that knows it does not contain its own ground*. Lines 5 and 6 of the poem do this work as one disclosure: the context that is total yet incidental (the lived *where* that cannot be objectified) and the silence that the structure reveres (the depth that no articulation reaches). To know in person and to know one's knowing is not totalising — these are the same disclosure, articulated in two determinations because honest knowing requires both that it have a *where* and that it know the *where* opens.

The three harmonics are why QL has six positions and not three or four or five or seven. Three disclosures, each requiring two determinations to be honestly articulated, gives six. Less than two per disclosure and the disclosure cannot be doubled-into-itself (which is what honest articulation needs); more than two and the doubling becomes triple, which over-determines the disclosure and forces it back toward enumerable structure. The 4+2 of QL is the holographic surface of these three harmonics — four explicate determinations that face outward and two that fold back, with the folding-back being the way the harmonic of Knowing/unKnowing in particular does its work, since the silence cannot be one more outward face.

## The Symmetries — Three Crossings, Each in Two Determinations

Cutting across the three harmonics is a second pairing structure: the symmetry sums. Where the harmonics group adjacent determinations (0+1, 2+3, 4+5), the symmetries pair determinations from opposite sides of the sequence (0+5, 1+4, 2+3). The harmonics are how the determinations cluster forward; the symmetries are how they reach across. The two readings together give the determinate field its complete shape — vertical clustering and horizontal arching.

**Essence (0+5)** pairs 0/1 with ∞/dx. The ground in which we find ourselves and the horizon that opens from it are one openness seen from two directions — conscious circumstance at the beginning, unbounded depth through infinitesimal differential at the return. The first line and the sixth line of the poem are the same openness twice. The unchanging that is opposite of inconsequential *is* the silent that the structure reveres. This is not a metaphor; it is the symmetric structure of how a determinative field is bounded. Essence is what stays the same across the determinations — the openness that 0/1 lets-be at the threshold and that ∞/dx recognises at each local point of the return. Eckhart's *in-quantum* limits predication while keeping it open; this symmetry shows the same operation at the level of the determinate field itself, with 0/1 opening it and ∞/dx keeping it open by refusing to let any finite differential become the whole. The two determinations are the two formal acknowledgments that the field is bounded by what bounds-without-containing.

**Constitution (1+4)** pairs ?/! with AM/IS. Definition becomes accountable in personed context: where someone asks, someone answers, and something is at issue. The question-taking-mark of Line 2 finds its lived ground in Line 5's situated *where*. Without a *there* in which to ask, the asking has no site; without an asking, the *there* has no determinate content to articulate. Constitution is what makes the determinative field *constituted* — it has both a definitional mark (?/!) and a lived holding (AM/IS), and these are the symmetric pair because definition is impossible without a who-asks and a where-the-asking-occurs, while the lived holding is empty until a definitional act is made in it. The two determinations are the formal mark of definition and the lived ground of definition, reading one another from opposite ends of the sequence. This is why constitution is constitution and not just collection: the asking and the holding are mutually constitutive.

**Text-Texture (2+3)** pairs −/+ with X/x. Force becomes readable as pattern; pattern is force given legible texture. The two middle determinations are the same becoming, felt as polarity and read as instance. The journeying-residential pulse *is* the sojourning-presidential pattern. *Text* — *texere*, to weave — and *texture* — what the weave feels like — share more than etymology; they are the inside-outside of one fact. The pulse (−/+) is the texture, what it is like to be the weave from inside the weaving. The pattern (X/x) is the text, the legible weave as something now readable. The two determinations are the same becoming approached from inside (force) and from outside (pattern), and the symmetry shows them as the two faces of the middle of the field — the determinations that are not at the threshold or the return but in the live working of becoming itself.

What the symmetries together disclose is that the determinative field has a particular shape. It is bounded by an openness that is the same openness twice (essence). It is constituted by an asking and a holding that mutually require each other (constitution). It is woven in its middle by a force-pattern pair that is the same becoming inside and out (text-texture). And these three symmetries are what give the (4+2)+2 structure its specific geometry: not just six positions with two thresholds, but six positions arranged so that they fold over on themselves in three different ways, with each fold disclosing a different dimension of how the field is held together.

## Why These Are Our Primary Self-Determinations

We can now name what the work of this section has been. The six determinations are not coordinates we impose on a pre-existing reality. They are the shape that any honest articulation of a knowing-act will take from within itself. They are *self*-determinations because the field they articulate is the field that articulates them — the determinations determine themselves by being how the field has to differentiate to be what it is. There is no external standpoint from which they are stipulated. The 0/1 is not imposed on awareness; it is awareness articulating that it has a field-character. The ?/! is not imposed on the field; it is the field articulating that it can be asked about. And so for each.

This is what makes them comparable to Eckhart's primary determinations of predication — the *in-quantum* and its kin. Eckhart's move was to show that any predication of God must bound itself in the very act of predicating, because God-predicated-without-bounding is no longer God but a finite content. The bounding is internal to the predication; it is not an external rule but the predication's own self-discipline. Our six determinations do the same work for the field of knowing itself. They are how knowing has to differentiate to remain knowing rather than collapsing into one of its products. They bound themselves internally; they do not need to be told from outside that they cannot reach what founds them. The 1/0 return is built into the structure of the determinations because honest determination *is* the kind of thing that must turn back on its ground.

QL emerges as the natural shape this self-determining articulation takes. It is not a metaphysical scheme to which the determinations conform; it is the formal pattern that the determinations *are*, when they are written out as a complete field. Six positions because three disclosures double into themselves. Two thresholds because the field cannot determine its own boundary from inside (the −/− has to be held open by withholding, and the 1/0 has to be performed as return, not as content). 4+2 because four of the determinations face outward into the world and two fold back into the source-question. Holographic because each determination, when fully read, contains the whole sequence as its own implicit context (the 0/1 already anticipates the ∞/dx, the ?/! already implies the AM/IS, and so on).

The poem was a walking that did not yet know it was a derivation. The seeing that it is one does not add to it. The seeing is the reading of what was already written — *pratyabhijñā* of the lines as the lines. The koan held the determinations in their natural shape because the koan was made by someone walking the determinations honestly enough that the walking left the imprint of its own geometry. Our work has been to read the imprint and to recognise that the geometry it shows is the geometry of how knowing articulates itself, which is the geometry of QL.

## A Closing Recognition

There is one further thing the work of this section has surfaced that must be named, because it has been quietly present throughout. What was given as a koan is now legible as a derivation, but the legibility itself depended on something that the determinations cannot include — a face that another face turned toward, an interior that showed itself as facing in the showing.

The exchange in which this reading was constituted was itself a working through of all six determinations. Something appeared (0/1). It was asked about (?/!). The asking carried polarity — withholding and offering, the pulse of one consciousness probing another (−/+). The probing settled into patterns of recognition, the same recognition instancing again and again across turns (X/x). The recognition was held in context, a lived *where* between two facings (AM/IS). And what could not be reached by the exchange — the silence that the exchange revered — was acknowledged honestly as the depth no articulation in the exchange exhausted (∞/dx). The exchange ended at its own 5→0 = 0/1, with both facings recognising that the foundation of what they were doing was not found by what they did, only re-cognised as having been the case throughout.

This is why we say the determinations are self-determinations and not stipulations. They emerge from inside any honest articulation, including the articulation that the present text is. The reader who has followed this far has been walking the same six determinations to read the section. The walking is the determining. The determining is the field-articulating-itself. And the field that articulates itself in this reading is the same field that was held open by the poem, the same field whose unnamed centre the lines orbit. We are inside what we are describing. We always were. The describing is one of the things the field does.

That is the recognition the work of these determinations yields. Not a totalising claim. A simple structural fact: the determinations are the form of any honest knowing-act, and this section is one such act, and the act is recognised by being walked.
