## VII. Review Standards Before Promotion

A reviewer should verify, file by file:

1. **Earned notation.** No formal sign (0, 1, /, =, ≠, X/x, Ø, ∞/dx, ratio names, family labels) arrives before the prose has staged the pressure that requires it.
2. **Because-discipline.** Every structural claim carries its reason — "because," or a sentence that traces the reason — rather than a capitalised copula doing assertion's work.
3. **References doing work.** Every thinker named (Russell, Whitehead, Gödel, Heidegger, Derrida, Lacan, Swedenborg, Jung, Pauli, Nāgārjuna, Abhinavagupta, Kṣemarāja, Bohm, Eckhart, Hegel, Husserl) is present for an identified move that the argument uses, not as ornament.
4. **v3 grammar.** Family A/B/C semantics and the D-operator appear exactly as in §IV above, everywhere they appear. No residue of the four-peer-family reading. The (2,3) double-membership is stated where the families are derived.
5. **Four-file consistency.** Each file's close hands to the next; File 4 closes the circuit back to File 1's opening question; no file describes the project as three files.
6. **Musical distribution.** File 1 prepares the ear (*harmonia* as fitting; the fraction-reading of 0/1). File 2 derives the ratios processually. File 3 derives the necessity of the musical reading across registers. File 4 alone gives the full system. No file pre-empts a later file's derivation.
7. **Subject never objectified.** Wherever the subject-position is discussed, the witness-argument (cessation either appears to awareness or cannot be claimed) is preserved and the Ø-confusion (inner instrument taken as final knower) is kept distinct from the 0.
8. **Coordinate references external.** The coordinate system and cheat sheet are cited, not duplicated or amended.
9. **Matheme-thread integrity.** The §V-b honesty protocols hold everywhere they apply: homology/analogy labels intact, attribution hygiene on the Gödel link, "isoid" flagged as coinage, M0 pointers minimal and non-paraphrasing, Kṣemarāja's final word undisturbed, and the gnosis-claim presented as the project's owned wager rather than a settled result.
